# Fact dump (user research, 30 Aug 2026) — condensed for slide writers

## Executive thesis

Aceh has Indonesia's broadest asymmetric-autonomy package (local parties, qanun + Islamic law, joint migas mgmt, extra DBH, foreign cooperation right, telecom provisions, Sabang FTZ, Otsus fund) but is a province inside a unitary state. Binding framework = UUD 1945 → UU 11/2006 (UUPA) → PP/Perpres → national sector laws → qanun. MoU Helsinki = political foundation; UUPA = enforceable instrument.
Core claim: Aceh can become Shenzhen-like in industrial execution and Dubai-like in zone development under powers it already has; it cannot become Singapore-like in sovereign powers without constitutional change. Obstacle ≠ "insufficient autonomy" but: incomplete UUPA implementation, overlap with later sector laws, weak provincial delivery institutions, fragmented land/infra/investment execution, transfer dependence.

## Helsinki MoU vs UUPA (promise → law)

| Area | MoU 2005 | UUPA 2006 |
| General authority | all public sectors except foreign affairs, defence, security, monetary/fiscal, justice, religion | Art 7 similar language; central functions + PP 3/2015 controlling |
| Consent | consultation AND consent of DPRA/Governor (1.1.2 b-d) | Art 8: consultation and CONSIDERATION only |
| Taxes | Aceh sets/raises taxes (1.3.2) | local taxes within national law; no own income/corporate/customs code |
| Borrowing | external loans (1.3.1) | foreign loans via central channels, MoF approval |
| Interest rates | differ from BI (1.3.1) | only negotiate lending rates / subsidize interest |
| Resources | 70% of hydrocarbons + all natural resources in territory + territorial sea (1.3.4) | oil 15%+55%, gas 30%+40% = 70% migas only; other resources per DBH formulas |
| Ports/airports | Aceh develops + administers all (1.3.5) | manage what Aceh builds; existing BUMN facilities joint mgmt/JV (Art 172-173) |
| Trade/access | free internal trade, direct sea/air access abroad (1.3.6-7) | national customs/aviation/immigration; Sabang customs exception only |
| Courts | independent Aceh court system incl. appellate, inside RI (1.4.3) | Mahkamah Syar'iyah under MA; no separate jurisdiction |
| Police/prosecutors | Aceh consent for chiefs, recruitment (1.4.4) | governor approval for provincial chiefs with fallback; Polri/Kejagung |
| HAM | Pengadilan HAM for Aceh (2.2), KKR (2.3) | Art 228-229; never established / KKR via qanun 2013 |

## Aceh-only powers

Local political parties (PP 20/2007) · qanun + Islamic law fields · Mahkamah Syar'iyah + Baitul Mal (zakat = PAD; zakat reduces PPh per Art 192) · Wali Nanggroe + adat · joint migas mgmt + BPMA (PP 23/2015) · 70% migas DBH · Otsus 2% DAU (yrs 1-15) 1% (16-20), ends 2027 · consultation on national measures (Perpres 75/2008) · foreign cooperation (Perpres 11/2010) · flag/emblem/hymn (not sovereignty symbols, Art 246).
vs Papua (MRP, Otsus, no local parties) · DIY (governor appointment, culture, land) · ordinary provinces (UU 23/2014).

## Telecom (UUPA Art 151/152/165)

Aceh may: license special telecom (non-spectrum), recommend on fixed cable networks, supervise services, coordinate USO, tower guidelines, cable excavation permits; central must prioritize rural telecom in Aceh (Art 152); local operator permits (Art 165). Spectrum/numbering = national. Model: BUMD owning open-access fiber, ducts, towers, fixed broadband, gov/school nets, rural backhaul, public wifi, Aceh IXP, data centers, disaster comms, industrial connectivity, wholesale to MNOs; mobile brand via MVNO. Asks: sandbox for Sabang/Arun, private 5G pilots, reconcile Art 151/165 with PP 46/2021, USO commitments, streamlined licence.

## Reform ladder

Tier 1 (now): Invest Aceh; Aceh Development Corp; two corridors (Arun–Lhokseumawe: fertilizer/chemicals/gas/food/assembly/RE equipment/data/logistics; Sabang–Banda: maritime/tourism/fisheries/transshipment/repair/digital/education-health); Aceh digital utility; convert Otsus into legacy assets (project prep, infra equity, endowment, maintenance reserves, vocational funds).
Tier 2: UUPA Implementation Register (implemented/partial/blocked/contradicted/awaiting transfer/awaiting ministry/needs litigation) → Central–Aceh Implementation Compact with deadlines.
Tier 3: fiscal compact (permanent post-2027 funding, assigned taxes, transparent central revenue calc, fiscal council); consent (timelines, published opinions, reasons, mediation; concurrence list); Jeju-style rolling devolution commission; Aceh Free Port & Industrial Transformation Act (Sabang+Arun+logistics zones, rules of origin, bonded movement, tax, customs, talent); sandboxes (private 5G, Islamic fintech, port/customs digital, microgrids, carbon/blue economy); commercial chamber + arbitration.
