### Telecom / digital regs

- PP 46/2021 (omnibus telecom). Licences via Komdigi OSS; Komdigi page lists BUMD as eligible for network licences. Relevant class: Jaringan Tetap Tertutup. Only precedent = passive-infra BUMD (Jakarta JIP/Jakpro: SJUT ducts, 5G smart poles, JakWifi). No active-network BUMD found.
- Spectrum: 700 MHz + 2.6 GHz auction winners 22 Jul 2026 (Telkomsel/Indosat/XLSmart; 5G ≥51% pop in 5 yrs). 3.5 GHz under study; 26 GHz designated, no auction. 5G coverage 4.44% residential. **No private-5G regime**; closest = Telekomunikasi Khusus licence + Komdigi community-internet sandbox (planning, Sep 2025).
- **Palapa Ring Barat does NOT cover Aceh** (Riau/Kepri only). Aceh backbone = Telkom only (ASBL subsea, Sabang-Aceh 44 km ⚠). SEA-ME-WE 5 lands at Dumai. Kepmen KP 14/2021: only 4 international landing sites (Jakarta, NTT, Manado, Jayapura) — Sabang excluded → hard blocker needing amendment. No commercial DC in Aceh. BAKTI: no new BTS 2025. IIX: 13 provinces, no Aceh node; path = APJII Aceh chapter + neutral colo.
- BUMD (PP 54/2017): Perumda vs Perseroda (≥51% region; outside shareholders ≤49%). Obligasi daerah: POJK 10/2024; **zero pemda issuances ever** (Jateng 2019, Jabar 2024-26 failed). KPBU: Perpres 38/2015 still in force; Peraturan LKPP 1/2025.
