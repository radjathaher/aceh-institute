Palapa Ring Barat stops at Riau and Kepri; Telkom is the only backbone. An open-access BUMD fiber utility, ducts along provincial roads, an Aceh IXP, a data centre, a Sabang landing station, LEO and direct-to-cell for the last villages, private 5G for Arun and Sabang, and later an MVNO brand.
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Ps 151–152 and 165 (special telecom licensing, USO coordination, local operator permits); PP 46/2021 and Komdigi rules list BUMD as eligible network licensees (Jaringan Tetap Tertutup class); precedent: Jakarta's JIP with SJUT ducts and 5G smart poles.
BLOCKER
Kepmen KP 14/2021 permits only four international landing sites (Jakarta, NTT, Manado, Jayapura), so Sabang needs an amendment; no private-5G regime exists and spectrum is national; no PP has ever implemented Ps 151/165; BAKTI built no new BTS in 2025.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
Charter the utility as a Perseroda and file the Jaringan Tetap Tertutup licence; grant duct rights along provincial roads; stand up an IXP node with the APJII Aceh chapter in neutral colocation; run a LEO or direct-to-cell pilot in the 149 villages; file the Sabang landing-site amendment and a private-5G sandbox ask. Fold PT PEMA's June 2026 telecom initiative into this utility rather than a fifteenth subsidiary.
KPI · 5 YEARS
Zero blank-spot villages; Aceh IXP live; one certified data centre operating; Sabang listed as an international landing site; one private-5G pilot in Arun or Sabang; wholesale fiber sold to at least two national operators.
SO WHAT
Passive infrastructure needs no permission Aceh does not already have; the licence class exists and BUMD are eligible. Only spectrum and the Sabang landing need Jakarta, so build the ducts and the exchange first and negotiate the rest from a position of owning the ground.
