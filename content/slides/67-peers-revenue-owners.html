09 · WHAT PEERS TEACH
S09-02
Revenue owners fund themselves: the Basque Country collects €18.3bn and pays Madrid 8%; Sarawak took 5% of petroleum.
Cluster A, the regions that own a tax base: Basque Country, Sarawak and Hong Kong; latest verified figures 2024–2026.
CLUSTER A · REVENUE OWNERS
BASQUE COUNTRY · CONCIERTO 12/2002
€18.31bn
Taxes collected by the Basque treasuries, 2024
Collects IRPF, CIT, VAT and excise itself; pays a negotiated cupo for state services. Limit: EU state-aid law (1995–2000 tax holidays ruled illegal).
SARAWAK · MA63, ART 1(2) 2021
RM19.55bn
5% state sales tax on petroleum, 2019–2024
Petros became sole gas aggregator on 1 Mar 2025
Own immigration control (Constitution Part VII)
Leverage came from ordinances plus litigation; MA63 has no oil clause
HONG KONG · BASIC LAW 1990
Art 106
Independent finances; no central tax on the region
Boundary comparator only: a separate customs territory (Art 116) and own courts sit outside what a unitary state can grant. Useful for the fiscal principle, not the constitutional form.
LESSON
Regions that own a revenue base negotiate from strength: they fund their own institutions and decide what to buy from the centre.
ACEH TRANSFER · OWN A REVENUE BASE
1
Assigned taxes in the fiscal compact
Move from a DAU percentage to a defined share of taxes raised in Aceh, calculated transparently (Basque model, unitary form).
2
A resource levy plus an aggregator
Sarawak's 5% petroleum SST and Petros show a state can earn from gas it does not own, if it controls the offtake.
3
Productive assets before rate cuts
The Basque tax-holiday defeat shows incentives get challenged; owned land, ports and utilities do not.
SO WHAT
Aceh's PAD is 39% of revenue; every revenue-owner peer is above 90%. The compact must move Aceh toward its own base, not a longer grant.
