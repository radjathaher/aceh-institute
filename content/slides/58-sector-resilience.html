S07-12
One flood erased a year of growth — Q4 2025 fell 1.61% — so every SPV project must pass a climate-risk screen before it is funded.
Five-lane summary for disaster resilience and climate: floods, the tsunami legacy, mangrove and blue carbon, early warning, resilient-infrastructure standards.
OPPORTUNITY
−1.61%
Q4 2025 GRDP year-on-year, after the November floods
−6.9%
Aceh exports 2025, which Kemendag attributes to the floods
Mangrove restoration with blue-carbon credits; early-warning and evacuation assets on a tsunami coast; resilient-infrastructure standards and a maintenance reserve inside the Legacy Fund.
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Art 188 (Aceh may hold a reserve fund for multi-year needs) and Art 156(3) (resource management under transparency and sustainable-development principles); Aceh's spatial-planning and qanun powers set building standards.
BLOCKER
BPK (Jun 2026) found stalled Otsus projects and weak monitoring; 2026 capital spending is only Rp576B, so maintenance is the first casualty; no project-preparation step screens for flood, tsunami or heat risk.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
A climate-risk screen on every SPV project before funding, a maintenance-reserve rule written into the Legacy Fund, and one mangrove concession pilot with carbon measurement and verification.
KPI · 5 YEARS
100% of SPV projects screened; 10,000 ha of mangrove under verified carbon accounting (target); every coastal gampong covered by early warning; disaster loss below 0.5% of GRDP in any year.
SO WHAT
Resilience is not a sector; it is a gate. A screen at the funding stage costs less than one quarter of contraction, and the mangrove line pays for itself in carbon.
