LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Ps 191–192 (zakat as PAD; zakat reduces income tax); qanun and Mahkamah Syar'iyah powers; MK decision 140/PUU-XXIII/2025 upholding Aceh's zakat management; POJK 10/2024 on regional bonds and sukuk; PP 54/2017 for BUMD issuers.
BLOCKER
The Ps 192 implementing regulation has been stuck at Kemendagri for two decades; PMK 114/2025 allows only a gross-income deduction, not a credit; no Islamic fintech sandbox regime exists; Jateng (2019) and Jabar (2024–26) both failed to bring a regional bond to market, so credit quality and a ring-fenced asset are the test.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
Sukuk daerah prospectus with OJK and BEI for one revenue asset (cold chain or the fiber utility); Baitul Mal chartered as co-investor with a published mandate; the zakat tax credit written into the RUU text now on the DPR table; halal park land option inside KEK Arun; formal Islamic fintech sandbox request to OJK.
KPI · 5 YEARS
First sukuk daerah listed and serviced on time; twenty halal-certified manufacturers in the two corridors; zakat collected through Baitul Mal rising year on year; sandbox granted and one licensed Islamic fintech operating from Aceh.
SO WHAT
Every other province can build a factory; only Aceh can offer a syariat jurisdiction with its own zakat law confirmed by the Constitutional Court. Being first to market with a sukuk daerah is a credibility signal Jakarta cannot give and cannot take away.
