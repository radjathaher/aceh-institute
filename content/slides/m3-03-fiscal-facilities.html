M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 166 FISCAL FACILITIES
M3-03
Article 166 lets Jakarta grant Aceh tax and customs relief on request; no PMK or PP has ever done so.
The mandate as written, the absence of any implementing instrument since 2006, and what the current revision leaves out.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Article 166
“The Government … may provide fiscal facilities in the form of tax relief, exemption from import duties, exemption from taxes on the import of capital goods and raw materials into Aceh and the export of finished goods from Aceh, investment facilities, and other fiscal facilities proposed by the Aceh Government.”
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
A Kemenkeu PMK or a PP on Aceh fiscal facilities (import duty, PPh and PPN relief on capital goods and raw materials), triggered by a formal Aceh Government proposal.
STATUS
BLOCKED
No instrument found in twenty years. Absence-of-evidence finding.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Kemenkeu (DJP, DJBC, BKF). The Aceh Government must table the proposal first; the article only responds to one.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
A costed Aceh proposal (a KEK Arun and Sabang capital-goods and raw-materials list) filed under Art 166, and an RUU clause that gives Kemenkeu a deadline to answer it.
SO WHAT
This is the cheapest lever in the register: the article needs no new law, only a proposal Aceh has never filed and a ministry that has never answered.
Source: UU 11/2006 Art 166; Dialeksis, revision article list; Informasi Publik, Jun 2026. Note: the no-instrument finding rests on one source. [1142][1108][1109]
