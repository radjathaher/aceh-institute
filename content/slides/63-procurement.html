S08-04
UUPA Art 195 lets Aceh write its own APBA procurement procedure — open contracting is the cheapest credibility Aceh can buy.
Before and after lanes: from paper tenders and stalled projects to open contracting data, framework agreements and independent project assurance.
DIMENSION
BEFORE · TODAY
AFTER · PERGUB UNDER ART 195
Tender data
Notices as PDFs on LPSE; no machine-readable record of who bid, who won and what was paid.
Open contracting data for every APBA contract: planning, tender, award, spend, one public dataset.
Documents
Bespoke terms for each project; disputes re-litigated every year; bidders price the uncertainty.
Standard project documents: one contract family for works, one for concessions, one for services.
Buying
One-off tenders per unit; the same road, school or server bought a dozen times at a dozen prices.
Framework agreements for repeat categories; call-offs by any Aceh unit at the framework price.
Contract form
Input-based: paid for progress on paper; stalled Otsus projects flagged by BPK in June 2026.
Outcome-based where it can be measured: paid on availability, uptime, throughput, or graduates placed.
Oversight
Internal inspectorate and after-the-fact audit; weak monitoring and evaluation noted by BPK.
Independent project assurance at gateway reviews before money moves; findings published with the register.
