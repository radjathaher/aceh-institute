10 · THE JAKARTA PACKAGE
S10-06
Eight sector asks, each with the instrument it needs and a precedent Jakarta has already accepted.
Telecom, customs, talent, ports and airports, land, zakat, fiscal facilities and human-rights institutions: instrument and precedent for each; 2026.
EIGHT ASKS · INSTRUMENT NEEDED · PRECEDENT
TELECOM
Reconcile Art 151/165 with PP 46/2021; Sabang landing station; private-5G and community-internet sandbox; USO.
INSTRUMENT
PP under Art 151/165; amend Kepmen KP 14/2021 (4 landing sites today)
PRECEDENT
Komdigi sandbox 2025; Jakarta JIP passive-infra BUMD
CUSTOMS
One bonded corridor Sabang–Arun with a single customs regime and rules of origin.
INSTRUMENT
PMK on bonded movement; zone chapter of the omnibus act
PRECEDENT
UU 37/2000 Sabang FTZ; Hainan island closure 18 Dec 2025
TALENT
Zone work-permit fast track for Sabang and Arun employers, with a published service standard.
INSTRUMENT
Kemnaker and Imigrasi zone rule; Invest Aceh as single window
PRECEDENT
Jeju visa-free entry; DIFC free-zone employment rules
PORTS / AIRPORTS
Execute Art 172–173: joint management and JVs for Pelindo and InJourney assets in Aceh.
INSTRUMENT
Kemenhub decree plus JV agreements; PN Ksp suit 6/Pdt.G/2025 pending
PRECEDENT
2026 RUU text already moves ports and airports to Aceh and kab/kota
LAND
Execute Perpres 23/2015: transfer Kanwil BPN into Badan Pertanahan Aceh (Art 253).
INSTRUMENT
ATR/BPN transition team; no new law needed
PRECEDENT
Perpres signed 12 Feb 2015; Kanwil still operating Jul 2026
ZAKAT
Issue the PP that makes zakat a PPh credit, as Art 192 has said for 20 years.
INSTRUMENT
PP (RPP stuck at Kemendagri); PMK 114/2025 gives only a gross-income deduction
PRECEDENT
MK 140/PUU-XXIII/2025 affirmed Aceh's zakat kekhususan
ART 166 FISCAL FACILITIES
The first-ever implementing rule for Art 166, tied to the Sabang–Arun perimeter.
INSTRUMENT
PMK or PP; the 2026 RUU does not touch Art 166
PRECEDENT
Hainan 15% CIT/IIT; Jeju IPZ tax holidays
HAM COURT / KKR
Form the Aceh human-rights court (Art 228) and protect Qanun KKR 17/2013 from revocation.
INSTRUMENT
Keppres for the court; withdraw the Kemendagri letter of 7 Nov 2024
PRECEDENT
MoU 2.2–2.3; KKR has recorded 10,652 violations
HOW THE ASKS ARE SEQUENCED
NO NEW LAW
Land, ports/airports, zakat PP, Art 166 rule, HAM court: execution of what UUPA already says.
MINISTERIAL
Landing-station amendment, talent fast track, bonded corridor: ministerial decrees.
OMNIBUS
Telecom reconciliation and sandboxes, zone tax and customs regime: chapters 3 and 5 of the act.
