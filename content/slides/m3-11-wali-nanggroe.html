M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 96–97 WALI NANGGROE
M3-11
The Wali Nanggroe institution exists and is funded; its legitimacy is contested at home, not in Jakarta.
Articles 96–97 mandate, the 2012–2013 qanun, and the third-term and budget disputes of 2023–2026.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Articles 96–97
Art 96(1): “The Wali Nanggroe institution is a customary leadership uniting society, independent, authoritative, and empowered to foster and supervise customary institutions, customs, titles and ceremonies.” Art 96(2): it is not a political or governmental institution.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
Qanun 8/2012 and Qanun 9/2013 are issued. Missing: term limits, an open succession procedure and a public budget line, all within Aceh's own qanun power.
STATUS
IMPLEMENTED
CONTESTED
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
DPRA and the institution itself (qanun revision); the Governor (APBA line). No central blocker.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
A qanun amendment with term limits, an open succession procedure and an annual public report. The fix is entirely within Aceh's own hands.
SO WHAT
The register shows Jakarta is not the only obstacle. An institution Aceh controls but cannot reform weakens the case that Aceh can run the powers it asks for.
