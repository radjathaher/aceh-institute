Ring-fencing 30% of Otsus into a legacy fund compounds to roughly Rp15–23 trillion by 2037 — an illustrative model, not a forecast.
Aceh Legacy Fund: inputs, vehicle, and 10-year projection 2028–2037 under two return scenarios (illustrative).
INPUTS / YEAR
30% of Otsus ≈ Rp1.2T (at Rp4.0T) · 25% of migas DBH ≈ Rp0.08T (2025 province DBH Rp327.8B) · Andaman gas revenue from 2029, assumed Rp0.5T/yr in the base case
VEHICLE
Perseroda under PP 54/2017 (Aceh ≥51%) · independent board, published mandate · Baitul Mal-style trustee governance and annual public audit
USES
Infrastructure equity in corridor SPVs · project preparation · maintenance reserves · vocational endowment
ASSUMPTIONS (ILLUSTRATIVE)
Conservative: Rp1.28T/yr, 4% net return, no Andaman revenue. Base: Rp1.28T/yr + Rp0.5T/yr from 2029, 6% net return. Nominal, before inflation; no withdrawals. Not a forecast.
ACEH LEGACY FUND — PROJECTED BALANCE, RP TRILLION, 2028–2037 (ILLUSTRATIVE)
SO WHAT
Otsus that is spent leaves bills; Otsus that is invested leaves a balance sheet. A fund makes the permanent 2.5% easier for Jakarta to defend, because the money visibly compounds.
