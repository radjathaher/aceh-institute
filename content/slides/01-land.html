M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 253 LAND
M3-01
Land administration was due to pass to Aceh by 2008; the national Kanwil BPN still runs it in 2026.
Article 253 mandate, the 2015 Presidential Regulation that should have executed it, and the trail of unmet deadlines since.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Article 253
“The Aceh regional office of the National Land Agency and the kabupaten/kota land offices become organs of the Aceh Government … no later than the start of fiscal year 2008.” Detailed rules by Presidential Regulation.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
Perpres 23/2015 (signed 12 Feb 2015) plus an ATR/BPN transition team and hand-over decrees for staff, assets and the land database.
STATUS
BLOCKED
Perpres issued, never executed. 18 years past the statutory deadline.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Ministry of ATR/BPN (Kanwil BPN Aceh reports to it); Kemendagri for the regional-organ side.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
A dated ministerial decree (SK) forming the ATR/BPN–Aceh transition team, with a hand-over inventory of staff, assets and the land database; the RUU UUPA revision can add a hard cut-over date.
SO WHAT
Every industrial-land and corridor plan in this deck depends on who signs the title. Until Article 253 executes, Aceh plans land it does not administer.
