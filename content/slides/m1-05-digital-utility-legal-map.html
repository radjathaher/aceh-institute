THE SPECIFIC BLOCKERS
Sabang cannot land an international cable. Kepmen KP 14/2021 names only Jakarta, NTT, Manado and Jayapura; Aceh needs an amendment before any subsea play.
No private-5G or local-spectrum regime. The closest doors are a Telsus licence and Komdigi's community-internet sandbox, still at planning stage (Sep 2025).
Ps 151 and 165 have no PP after twenty years; the RUU UUPA touches Ps 165, but the fix must reconcile with PP 46/2021 or Komdigi will keep ignoring it.
PRECEDENT THAT WORKS TODAY
Jakarta's JIP (Jakpro) builds shared ducts (SJUT), 5G smart poles and JakWifi as a passive-infrastructure BUMD under the same national law.
SO WHAT
Do not wait for the PP. Ducts, dark fibre and a closed-network licence are available now; use them to earn the landing-site and spectrum asks.
