Sabang gets cruise calls and divers but no operator — tourism is 82% of its investment, yet the zone returns 1% of what the state put in.
Five-lane summary for tourism: Weh island dive and cruise, Gayo highland coffee, and a halal-certified destination brand.

Weh island dive and cruise calls (Crystal Symphony 2025; Azamara Dec 2025 and Apr 2026 [verify]); Gayo coffee tourism on a crop of ~35k t, 80% exported to 17 countries; one halal-certified destination brand.
LEGAL BASIS
UU 37/2000 (Sabang free trade zone) and UUPA Art 169 (Sabang developed jointly for trade, maritime, transport and tourism); UUPA Art 189 (equity and joint ventures with private operators, set by qanun).
BLOCKER
Thin air and sea access; BPKS has no national parent body since the Dewan Kawasan lapsed, unresolved land status, and a 2026 budget of Rp36.4B; tourism share of investment is declining.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
One destination-management concession for Weh: a single operator for marina, dive moorings, cruise tendering and waste, 25-year term, held through the Tourism Assets SPV.
KPI · 5 YEARS
At least 12 cruise calls a year; international arrivals tripled from the 2025 base; concession income above Rp150B a year (target); one scheduled international air link to Sabang or Banda Aceh.
SO WHAT
Sabang does not need another masterplan; it needs one operator with a 25-year contract and a reason to fill the ferry and the runway.
