02 · WHAT HELSINKI PROMISED
S02-04
Eight economic promises: 70% of all resources, every port and airport, own taxes and own borrowing.
Clauses 1.3.1–1.3.8 of the MoU, paraphrased from the official Indonesian text; clause 1.3.9 (BRR seats) omitted as spent.
1.3.1
Borrowing and interest rates
Aceh may raise funds through external loans, and may set interest rates different from those set by Bank Indonesia.
1.3.2
Taxes, trade, direct investment
Aceh may set and levy regional taxes; trade internally and internationally; attract foreign investment and tourists directly.
1.3.3
Living resources of the sea
Authority over the living natural resources in the territorial sea surrounding Aceh.
1.3.4
70% of all natural resources
70% of revenue from all current and future hydrocarbon reserves and other natural resources in Aceh and in the territorial sea around Aceh.
1.3.5
All ports and airports
Aceh develops and administers all sea ports and airports within its territory.
1.3.6
Free internal trade
Free trade with all parts of the Republic of Indonesia without taxes, tariffs or other barriers.
1.3.7
Direct foreign access
Direct and unhindered access to foreign countries by sea and by air.
1.3.8
Revenue transparency
Transparent collection and allocation of revenue between Jakarta and Aceh, verified by external auditors who report to the Head of the Aceh Government.
