03 · WHAT UUPA ENACTED
S03-05
Jakarta has already granted Papua a special-autonomy fund to 2041 — Aceh's ends in 2027, on a lower rate.
Three asymmetric regimes side by side: legal basis, fiscal instrument, representative institution, and the precedent each gives Aceh.
Papua
UU 2/2021
FISCAL
Otsus fund at 2.25% of the national DAU, running to 2041.
INSTITUTION
Majelis Rakyat Papua as cultural-representative chamber. No local parties.
PRECEDENT FOR ACEH
A 20-year fiscal horizon granted in 2021, after the first period lapsed. Jakarta renews special funds; the date is negotiable.
Yogyakarta
UU 13/2012
FISCAL
Keistimewaan funding through the national budget, no sunset clause [verify].
INSTITUTION
Governor by appointment of the Sultan; special powers over culture and Sultanate land.
PRECEDENT FOR ACEH
Kekhususan can be permanent and structural, not a programme with an end date.
Aceh
UU 11/2006 · revision at DPR since 26 May 2026
FISCAL
Otsus 2% of DAU 2008–22, 1% 2023–27; ends 2027. Draft revision: 2.5% of DAU — 20 years (Baleg) or permanent (DPRA).
INSTITUTION
Wali Nanggroe, local parties, statutory consultation right on national measures.
THE ASK
Papua's horizon plus Yogyakarta's permanence: 2.5% of DAU with no sunset, earmarked and audited.
SPECIAL-AUTONOMY FUND HORIZON
SO WHAT
Papua's 2041 horizon is the precedent to cite: Jakarta has already accepted that a peace settlement can carry a multi-decade fiscal obligation. Aceh asks for the same treatment, made permanent.
Source: UU 2/2021 (Papua); UU 13/2012 (DIY); UU 11/2006 Ps 183; Aceh Institute policy paper, Sept 2025; Kompas, 26 May 2026. [112][113][102][120][118]
