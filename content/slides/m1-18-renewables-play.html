The play is the tariff and the wire, not more plants: an Aceh utility with its own business area at Arun, gas from Andaman, and renewables sold direct to industry.
Phased play with owner institution, capital order-of-magnitude (estimates) and proposed KPIs, 2026–2031.
PHASE 1 · 2026–2027
Utility on paper
OWNER
Pemerintah Aceh + PT PEMA / Aceh Development Corp
CAPITAL
~Rp100B — studies, licensing, company set-up (estimate)
— Business-area application for KEK Arun
— Gas allocation MoU with BPMA and Mubadala
— Industrial tariff and demand study signed by tenants
PHASE 2 · 2028–2029
First wire
OWNER
Aceh utility + PLN + KEK Arun tenants
CAPITAL
Rp2–4T — plant, substation, tenant lines (estimate)
— 100–200 MW gas–solar hybrid at Arun with tenant PPAs
— Microgrid sandbox at Sabang and Simeulue
— One data-centre anchor tenant on a contracted tariff
PHASE 3 · 2030–2031
Sell the surplus
OWNER
Aceh utility + independent power producers
CAPITAL
Rp5–10T private renewables (estimate)
— 300 MW renewables: wind, solar, geothermal [verify]
— Green-industrial tariff for exporters in the zones
— Interconnection sales to North Sumatra
