M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · SINGAPORE EDB / JTC
M2-08-0
Singapore is an execution benchmark, not a sovereignty benchmark: EDB (1961) and JTC (1968) are statutory boards, not autonomy.
Statutory basis and mandate of EDB and JTC, 1961–2024. Sovereign powers are out of scope for a province.
STATUTORY BASIS
1961
Economic Development Board Act
EDB set up on 1 Aug 1961 as a statutory board: one window for investors, with a board, a chief executive and audited accounts.
1965
Constitution of the Republic of Singapore
Full sovereign powers: tax, customs, immigration, currency, courts. This is a state, not a devolved region.
1968
Jurong Town Corporation Act
JTC split out of EDB to own, service and lease industrial land and ready-built factories.
2024
EDB Year in Review
S$13.5bn fixed-asset investment, 18,700 jobs, 22 offices worldwide; JTC portfolio about 7,000 ha.
WHAT IS BENCHMARKED HERE, AND WHAT IS NOT
Investor account manager
Execution: one officer owns the investor end to end
Serviced land bank
Execution: JTC leases plots and factories, not permits
Statutory-board governance
Execution: board, CEO, annual report, audit
Tax, customs, immigration
Sovereign: not a benchmark for a province
Currency, courts, treaties
Sovereign: outside UUD 1945 for any region
SO WHAT
Copy the institution, not the country. EDB and JTC were created by ordinary statute; Aceh can create their equivalents by qanun under UUPA Ps 156 and 189 without a single new power from Jakarta.
