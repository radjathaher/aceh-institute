FOUR ASKS · MoU 1.3.4 AS THE TEXT
"70% of the revenues from current and future hydrocarbon deposits and other natural resources in the territory of Aceh as well as the territorial sea surrounding Aceh." Today: 70% of oil and gas only, onshore and to 12 nm.
01
12 → 200 nm co-management
BPMA becomes co-manager with SKK Migas for Aceh-offshore blocks; the May 2026 MoU gives coordination only.
02
DBH parity beyond 12 nm
Attribute Andaman production to Aceh so the 70% share applies regardless of distance from shore.
03
Onshore processing at Arun
Gas to the ORF and KEK Arun rather than an FPSO export; the Governor asked ESDM to hold the POD in Jun 2026 for this reason.
04
Extend the 70% to other resources
Minerals, forestry, fisheries and carbon, as the MoU's "other natural resources" reads; UUPA left these on national DBH formulas.
SO WHAT · PRECEDENT
Sarawak earned RM19.55bn from a 5% petroleum levy and made Petros the gas aggregator without owning the reserves. Aceh's ask is smaller than what Malaysia already conceded.
