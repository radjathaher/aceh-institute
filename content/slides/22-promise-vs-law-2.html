03 · WHAT UUPA ENACTED
S03-03
70% of everything became 70% of oil and gas; all ports became the ports Aceh builds; courts and police stayed national.
Four more lanes: resources, ports and airports, courts, police and prosecutors — MoU clause left, UUPA article right.

GAP

1.3.4 · RESOURCES
70% of revenue from all present and future hydrocarbon reserves and other natural resources, on land and in the territorial sea around Aceh.
migas only · 12 nm
Ps 181 (1)(b), (3) · PP 23/2015
Oil 15% + 55% and gas 30% + 40% = 70% of migas; forestry, fisheries, mining and geothermal at the general 80% DBH formula. Joint management through BPMA stops at 12 nautical miles.
1.3.5 · PORTS AND AIRPORTS
"Aceh melaksanakan pembangunan dan pengelolaan semua pelabuhan laut dan pelabuhan udara dalam wilayah Aceh."
all → own-built + JV
Ps 172–173
Aceh manages the ports and airports it builds; BUMN-run facilities are "dikerjasamakan", possibly as joint ventures. Never executed: LBH Kantara sued the President and Transport Minister, 24 Sep 2025.
1.4.3 · COURTS
An impartial and independent court system, including a court of appeals, established for Aceh within the Indonesian judicial system.
system → one court
Ps 128
Mahkamah Syar'iyah as part of the national religious-court branch under the Supreme Court, for Muslims in Aceh, in family, civil and jinayah matters. General courts and appeals unchanged.
1.4.4 · POLICE AND PROSECUTORS
Appointment of the Aceh police chief and chief prosecutor requires the consent of the Head of Aceh Government; recruitment and training with consent.
consent with fallback
Ps 205–206
Kapolri appoints with Governor approval; silence for 14 working days counts as approval; one refusal, then a second candidate; interim chief in urgent cases. Police remain part of Polri.

SO WHAT
Resources and ports are the two lanes with money attached — and the two where the 2026 revision draft already moves (joint migas management; ports and airports to Aceh and kab/kota). Courts and police are constitutional-level asks.
