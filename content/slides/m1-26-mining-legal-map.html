WHAT ACEH CAN DO NOW
Pass a Qanun on the Art 159 levy with audited disbursement and a public register of payers.
Make a community-benefit and local-supplier standard a condition of provincial land, roads and permits.
Use Perpres 75/2008 consultation to put the PP 3/2015 and UU 3/2020 conflicts on the record alongside the RUU UUPA.
SO WHAT
The province cannot win the licensing fight quickly. It can win the standards fight now: whoever holds the IUP still needs Aceh's land, roads, workers and levy compliance.
