Helsinki settled six things in ten pages — governance, rights, amnesty, security, monitoring and disputes.
The six sections of the Memorandum of Understanding signed in Helsinki on 15 August 2005, with the number of clauses under each.
SO WHAT
Thirty-nine of the seventy-one clauses were one-off peace mechanics, completed by 2006. The thirty-two clauses of Sections 1 and 2 are the standing promises this deck audits.
