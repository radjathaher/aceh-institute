M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · PAPUA
M2-09-2
The Papua precedent is Aceh's strongest domestic argument: the same legislator has already accepted a 2.25% share to 2041.
What the Papua model proves, what it warns against, and how Aceh uses it in the RUU UUPA negotiation.
WHAT PAPUA PROVES
Jakarta will extend a 20-year Otsus past its sunset: 2021 became 2041 by UU amendment.
Jakarta will raise the share, not only prolong it: 2% became 2.25%.
The instrument was a centrally initiated UU, which is exactly what the Baleg initiative of 26 May 2026 is.
WHAT PAPUA WARNS
An extension without delivery institutions repeats the input problem. Aceh's own BPK audit (Jun 2026) found stalled Otsus projects and weak monitoring after Rp104.23T.
Papua's package trades political autonomy for a cultural body: no local parties. Aceh should not accept money in exchange for instruments it already holds.
SO WHAT
Ask for the Papua clock, 2041 or permanent, and attach Aceh's own delivery conditions before Jakarta attaches its own.
