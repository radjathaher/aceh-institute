STATUTE
Special Act 2006 — Jeju Special Self-Governing Province
One national Act, amended in seven "institutional improvement" rounds. Each round moves a batch of central powers to the island; the seventh passed on 21 Jun 2023.
TIMELINE, 2006–2024
WHAT WAS DEVOLVED
4,660 central powers, moved in scheduled batches; own tax ordinances; Investment Promotion Zones with corporate-tax holidays.
Visa-free entry for visitors and an autonomous police force — powers most provinces never receive.
Each round still needs a National Assembly amendment; Jeju does not set the pace of its own devolution.
SO WHAT
Jeju's design is a conveyor, not a single grant: powers move in scheduled batches under one Act. Aceh's UUPA moved once in 2006 and then stalled on the PPs it needed.
