CLUSTER C · GRANT DEPENDENTS AND SLOW DEVOLVERS
BARMM · RA 11054 (2018) · BLOCK GRANT 5% OF NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE
PHP 337.5bn
block grant received, 2020–2024
4×
first parliamentary election postponed
Fiscal autonomy arrived before an elected parliament, a civil service or a project pipeline. Money without institutions produced low utilisation and a legitimacy gap.
JEJU · SPECIAL SELF-GOVERNING PROVINCE ACT 2006
4,660
powers transferred, item by item
7 rounds
of devolution, 2006 to 21 Jun 2023
Growth came, but every power needed its own negotiation. After 17 years Jeju itself asks Seoul for a single comprehensive transfer instead of the next list.
LESSON
Money without institutions stalls, and item-by-item devolution stalls too. Build the institutions first, then ask for powers in packages.
ACEH TRANSFER · OMNIBUS + INSTITUTIONS FIRST
1
Delivery unit and register before new money
BARMM shows a grant without institutions yields low utilisation. Aceh's Otsus record (BPK 2026: stalled projects, weak monitoring) rhymes with it.
2
Ask in packages, not items
Jeju needed seven rounds for 4,660 items. One omnibus act with a standing devolution commission avoids a 19-month wait per power.
3
Keep the political calendar off the critical path
BARMM's four postponements froze delivery. Aceh's institutions should be Pergub and Qanun bodies that survive electoral cycles.
SO WHAT
Aceh has spent 18 years in cluster C. The way out is the order of operations: institutions in 100 days, a compact in a year, an omnibus act in two.
