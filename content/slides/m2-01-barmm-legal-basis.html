The Bangsamoro got a statute, a parliament and an automatic block grant in 2018 — but the Supreme Court drew its map and Manila sets its election date.
Legal basis of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao: RA 11054 (2018) and what followed, 2018–2026.
STATUTE
RA 11054 (2018) — Bangsamoro Organic Law
Upheld by the Supreme Court on 9 Sep 2024, which also declared Sulu not part of the region. Block grant fixed at 5% of net national internal revenue.
TIMELINE, 2018–2026
WHAT WAS DEVOLVED
Bangsamoro Parliament and Chief Minister; Shari'ah courts inside the national judiciary.
Annual Block Grant: 5% of net national internal revenue, released automatically, plus a share of customs.
Not devolved: election timing, customs, immigration, and the map itself (Sulu removed by the Court in 2024).
SO WHAT
A statute plus an automatic grant is the Bangsamoro floor. Aceh has had both since 2006 and 2008 — its gap is execution and consent, not paper.
