M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 75–95 LOCAL PARTIES
M3-10
Local political parties are the register's clearest success: implemented in 2007 and holding 26 of 81 DPRA seats.
Articles 75–95 mandate, PP 20/2007, and the seat count of the largest local party across four elections.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Articles 75–95
Art 75(1): “Residents of Aceh may form local political parties.” The following articles set registration, membership, funding and the right to contest DPRA, DPRK and executive elections.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
PP 20/2007 on local political parties in Aceh. Issued within the two-year window and in force since.
STATUS
IMPLEMENTED
Four elections contested; 26 of 81 DPRA seats held by local parties.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Kemenkum (party registration); KIP Aceh (elections). No blocker; the risk is dilution in a future national party law.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE · PARTAI ACEH SEATS IN THE DPRA
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
Nothing to unblock. Defend the space: keep the local-party articles out of any national party-law consolidation while the RUU is open.
SO WHAT
The one mandate Jakarta implemented on time is the one that changed who sits in the room. Delivery, not autonomy design, is now the local parties' test.
