M1 · SECTOR PLAYBOOK · CREATIVE AND DIGITAL
M1-11-2
The digital play is a utility, a talent hub and a diaspora window: fibre first, then colocation, then a Sabang landing.
Three-phase play with owner, capital order of magnitude and KPIs, 2026–2031. Targets are proposals.
THREE-PHASE PLAY · OWNER · CAPITAL (ORDER OF MAGNITUDE, ESTIMATE)
PHASE 1 · 2026–27
Utility and hub
Owner: Diskominsa with Invest Aceh
~Rp100–300B, mostly public
Aceh Digital Utility chartered as a Perseroda with a fixed-network licence from Komdigi.
Banda Aceh talent hub and coworking anchor; Garuda Spark partner-city status [verify].
Diaspora investment window opened through the 49% outside-shareholder room.
PHASE 2 · 2028–29
Fibre and exchange
Owner: Digital Utility SPV
~Rp1–2T, public and private
Open-access fibre corridor Banda Aceh–Lhokseumawe with ducts and shared towers.
Neutral colocation and an IIX Aceh node; BPO and remote-work pilots with three anchor employers.
149 blank-spot villages closed with BAKTI and the community-internet sandbox.
PHASE 3 · 2030–31
Landing and data
Owner: Digital Utility SPV with a partner
~Rp2–5T, mostly private
Data centre at Arun or Sabang on gas-backed power from the Andaman fields.
Sabang international cable landing, which needs a Kepmen KP 14/2021 amendment.
Private-5G sandbox at Arun for industrial connectivity.
