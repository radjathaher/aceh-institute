M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 156 SDA AND MINING
M3-08
Aceh's natural-resource authority is real on paper and contested every time Jakarta recentralises mining.
Article 156 mandate, PP 3/2015 and the 2020 Minerba law, and the 2022 licence revocations that exposed the overlap.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Article 156
“The Aceh Government and kabupaten/kota governments manage natural resources in Aceh, on land and at sea within Aceh's area, according to their authority.” Covers minerals, coal, geothermal, forestry, agriculture, fisheries and marine; Art 165 adds the licence to issue mining permits.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
A harmonising rule between UU 3/2020 (Minerba) and PP 3/2015 on one side and UUPA Art 156/165 on the other: a Kepmen ESDM delegating IUP issuance for Aceh and OSS routing that recognises an Aceh licence.
STATUS
CONTESTED
Overlapping laws; both sides issue and revoke.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
ESDM (Minerba); BKPM / Kementerian Investasi (OSS); Setneg for PP 3/2015.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
A joint ESDM–Aceh licensing protocol (who issues, who revokes, one shared data room), and a test case asking the Constitutional Court to read UU 3/2020 against UUPA as lex specialis.
SO WHAT
Contested authority is worse than no authority: investors face two licensors and two revokers. Clarity, even if it concedes PMA to Jakarta, is the bankable outcome.
