Production autonomy compounds and transfer autonomy leaks: the difference is whether Otsus money buys assets or consumption.
Left: the self-reinforcing loop this deck proposes. Right: today's linear transfer chain, which ends at the 2027 Otsus cliff.
PRODUCTION AUTONOMY · THE LOOP THIS DECK PROPOSES
Legacy Fund inputs: post-2027 Otsus formula, 70% migas DBH, SPV dividends. Mandate: project preparation, infrastructure equity, endowment, maintenance reserves, vocational funds.
TRANSFER AUTONOMY · TODAY'S LINEAR CHAIN

77%

Rp576B

BPK 2026
stalled projects, weak monitoring; assets that do not earn, so nothing returns to the top of the chain
2027
Otsus at 1% of DAU ends; RUU offers 2.5% for 20 years or permanent — the chain restarts, the leak does not close
SO WHAT
Same money, different plumbing. Routing the post-2027 formula through a Legacy Fund with an asset mandate turns a cliff into a compounding base; the RUU is the moment to write that mandate into law.
