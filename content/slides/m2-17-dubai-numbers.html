One integrated port-and-zone operator produces over a third of the emirate's output. The value sits in the combination, not in either asset alone.
THE LIMIT: NOVEMBER 2009
Dubai World, the emirate's flagship holding, asked creditors for a standstill on about US$26bn of debt. The zones kept trading; the sovereign balance sheet did not, and federal support closed the gap.
Zone autonomy never included sovereign credit. Growth was borrowed against assets the zone model had made valuable.
SO WHAT
Zones create firms and trade, not fiscal immunity. Aceh's zone vehicles need a written debt ceiling and a named backstop from day one, because a province cannot restructure its way out.
