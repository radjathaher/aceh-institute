LESSON
A defined legal or customs perimeter attracts capital because investors can price the rules; the perimeter is an act of the centre, earned by execution.
ACEH TRANSFER · A SABANG + ARUN ZONE ACT
1
One customs perimeter, two nodes
Sabang FTZ (UU 37/2000) and KEK Arun (PP 5/2017) already exist; the act joins them with a bonded corridor and rules of origin (Hainan's 30% test).
2
A commercial chamber and arbitration seat
Not DIFC's separate courts, which a unitary state cannot grant, but a specialised chamber plus a recognised arbitration seat inside the zone.
3
Packaged, revocable empowerment
Ask for a Shenzhen-style list of reform items with a sunset and review, so Jakarta keeps a lever and Aceh earns the next package.
SO WHAT
Aceh has the zones but not the perimeter. A single act that fixes tax, customs, talent and dispute rules for Sabang and Arun is the Hainan move inside Indonesian law.
