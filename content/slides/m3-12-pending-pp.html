THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Articles 270–271
Art 271: “Implementing provisions of this Law that are the obligation of the Government shall be established no later than two years after this Law is promulgated.” Art 270: national-level rules by legislation; Aceh-level rules by qanun.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
The outstanding PP themselves. This register alone points to four: fiscal facilities (166), zakat credit (192), telecom (151/165) and port/airport JV rules (173). Definitive list [verify].
STATUS
PARTIAL
5 of 9 PP and 3 of 3 Perpres issued by 2019; deadline was 2008.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Setneg and Kemenkum (PP drafting programme); each sector ministry drafts its own.

WHAT UNBLOCKS IT

SO WHAT
Most red rows in this register are one missing regulation, not one missing law. The revision should legislate the calendar, not just the powers.
