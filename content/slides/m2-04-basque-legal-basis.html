M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · BASQUE COUNTRY
M2-02-0
The Basque Country collects its own taxes under the Concierto Económico and pays Madrid a quota — the inverse of Aceh's transfer model.
Legal basis of the Economic Agreement: Law 12/2002, the five-year cupo Law 10/2023, and the EU state-aid limit, 2002–2026.
STATUTE
Law 12/2002 — Concierto Económico
The Basque provinces collect the main taxes and pay a quota (cupo) for services Madrid still provides. The cupo is fixed by a five-year law, latest Law 10/2023.
TIMELINE, 2002–2026
WHAT WAS DEVOLVED
Provincial treasuries collect personal income tax, corporate tax, VAT and excise; own tax ordinances (normativa foral).
The region pays the State, not the reverse: cupo negotiated and fixed for five years by national law.
Not devolved: customs and immigration (EU), and any tax break that breaches EU state-aid rules.
SO WHAT
The Concierto is "collect first, pay up" — the strongest fiscal autonomy in a unitary state. It is a constitutional design, not an administrative grant, and that is the ask Aceh should frame for 2027.
