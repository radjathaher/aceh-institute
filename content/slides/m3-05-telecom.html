M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 151/165 TELECOM
M3-05
Aceh holds a telecom licensing power on paper that every operator still exercises through Komdigi.
Articles 151 and 165 mandates, the omnibus PP 46/2021 that routes licences through OSS, and the Aceh PP that was never written.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Articles 151 and 165
Art 151(2): Aceh may license special telecommunications “as long as it does not use radio frequency spectrum”, recommend on province-wide fixed cable networks, coordinate USO and set tower guidelines. Art 165: local investment and operator permits.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
A PP reconciling Art 151/165 with PP 46/2021 (delegated licence classes, BUMD network licence, USO commitments), or a Komdigi rule delegating the Telekomunikasi Khusus class.
STATUS
BLOCKED
No PP; every licence runs through the Komdigi OSS portal.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Komdigi (Dittel licensing, spectrum); BAKTI and Kemenkeu for USO funds.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
An Aceh proposal under Art 8 for a Komdigi delegation rule; in parallel, a BUMD passive-network licence (Jaringan Tetap Tertutup) that needs no delegation at all.
SO WHAT
The fibre utility in the M1 playbook does not wait for this PP; the Aceh-issued licence layer does. Build on the open door while lobbying for the closed one.
