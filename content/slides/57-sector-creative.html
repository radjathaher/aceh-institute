Digital work needs no port and no pipeline — but 149 villages are still blank spots and Aceh has no data centre and no exchange point.
Five-lane summary for the creative and digital economy: software, BPO and content, remote work, diaspora capital and skills, Banda Aceh as the hub.
OPPORTUNITY
149
blank-spot villages, Mar 2025 (536 with no internet in 2022)
0
commercial data centres and internet exchange nodes in Aceh
Software, BPO and content exports; remote work for Malaysian and Gulf clients; diaspora capital and skills; Banda Aceh as the hub city (Garuda Spark partner [verify]); Satu Data Aceh live since 29 Apr 2025.
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Art 151-152 (Aceh may license special telecom, coordinate universal service; the centre must prioritise rural telecom in Aceh) and Art 165; Perpres 11/2010 (foreign cooperation); digital transformation is one of the ten 2027 priorities.
BLOCKER
Palapa Ring Barat stops at Riau and Kepri; Kepmen KP 14/2021 lists only four international cable landing sites and Sabang is not one; Art 151/165 has no implementing PP, so every licence still runs through Komdigi.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
A diaspora investment window (one desk inside Invest Aceh, a matching fund, a published deal list) and a digital talent hub in Banda Aceh with a BPO anchor tenant and an APJII Aceh exchange node.
KPI · 5 YEARS
3,000 digital-export jobs (target); Aceh internet exchange live; zero blank-spot villages; at least 50 diaspora-backed firms registered through the window.
SO WHAT
Diaspora money follows a door, not a slogan. Open one desk, one hub and one exchange node, and the sector that needs no Jakarta permit can start this year.
