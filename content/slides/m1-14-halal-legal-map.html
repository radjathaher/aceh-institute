THE ASK, WITH PRECEDENT
1
Sign the Art 192 PP inside the RUU UUPA package by end-2026. Precedent: MK 140/2025 already affirmed Aceh's zakat kekhususan.
2
First sukuk daerah, Rp0.5–1T, on a revenue asset. Precedent: POJK 10/2024 permits it; OJK and BEI are courting issuers (May 2026).
3
An Aceh cell of the OJK innovation sandbox for Islamic fintech. Precedent: Komdigi's community-internet sandbox model (2025).
SO WHAT
Four of five UUPA articles already work. The block is one PP and one first-mover decision — both cheap, both signal-heavy.
