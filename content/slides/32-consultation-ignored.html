RULE EXISTS

Art 254 stripped district port powers Aceh's districts held under the UUPA; now litigated by a Simeulue council member
NOT CONSULTED

NOT CONSULTED

UU 3/2020 on Minerals and Coal
Recentralised mining licences; Aceh's letters asking Jakarta to honour its kekhususan were still unanswered in 2022
UNANSWERED

Aceh permits revoked centrally without the provincial step the UUPA provides; Aceh argued the revocations should be annulled

RUU 2026 TEXT ON ARTICLE 8
Consultation procedure moves to a Perpres plus a DPR regulation. Aceh's view remains a “consideration” — not the consent the MoU (1.1.2) promised.
SO WHAT
Consultation without a deadline, a published reply, or a concurrence list is a courtesy. The ask is procedural, cheap, and precedented: timelines, reasons in writing, mediation.
