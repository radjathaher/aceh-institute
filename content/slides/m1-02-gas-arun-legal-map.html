THE SPECIFIC BLOCKERS
The 12 nm line. Layaran and Tangkulo are deep-water finds outside it, so SKK Migas and ESDM hold the contract; the BPMA–SKK Migas MoU of 21 May 2026 gives Aceh coordination, not authority.
Processing location. POD-I (9 Mar 2026) approves an FPSO offshore; the Governor asked ESDM in June 2026 to delay the POD and land the gas at Arun. ESDM decides.
Revenue. The 70% DBH is secure only for production inside Aceh's territory; the offshore split beyond 12 nm follows the national formula unless the RUU UUPA fixes it.
ASK, WITH PRECEDENT
Extend joint management from 12 to 200 nm (Aceh proposal, Jun 2026) and set a provincial gas aggregator, as Sarawak did with Petros from 1 March 2025.
SO WHAT
Every instrument Aceh needs exists on paper. The contest is one line, 12 nm, and one decision, onshore or FPSO. Both sit with ESDM, so the play must open in Jakarta.
