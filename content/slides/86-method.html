S12-03
Method: research cut off on 30 Aug 2026, three verification tiers, and unverified claims left out rather than footnoted.
How the evidence was gathered, ranked and excluded; how to send corrections.
DATA CUTOFF AND SCOPE
30 Aug 2026
Nothing published after this date is reflected. Figures cover 2023 to H1 2026; the latest fiscal year is APBA 2026 as enacted.
SCANNED SYSTEMATICALLY
BPS and BPS Aceh releases · DJPK transfer decrees · JDIH (peraturan.go.id, peraturan.bpk.go.id) · DPMPTSP Aceh · DPR and Baleg record · Mahkamah Konstitusi · BPK audit summaries as reported · the Helsinki MoU text and UU 11/2006 article by article.
VERIFICATION TIERS
Tier 1 — primary release
Official statistical release, gazetted law, court ruling, agency report. Used without qualification.
Tier 2 — news of an official statement
A named official or agency quoted by a credible outlet. Used, attributed as "via" the outlet in the footer.
Tier 3 — unverified
Single-source, anonymous or unresolvable. Excluded from the deck entirely, not footnoted.
RULE
Where two tiers disagree, the slide shows the range and both sources.
LEFT OUT ON PURPOSE
01
The Rp108.7T Otsus cumulative variant — only Rp104.23T is on record (Kemenko Polkam).
02
Aceh internet-penetration figure (75.65%) — no primary release found.
03
Whether the Surpres and DIM for the RUU UUPA have been issued — unconfirmed at cutoff.
04
A definitive list of UUPA implementing regulations still missing — counts differ by source.
05
Any claim that attributes blame to a named individual.
