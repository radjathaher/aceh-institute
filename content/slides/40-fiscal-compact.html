05 · OTSUS AND THE 2027 CLIFF
S05-06
Peers that own their revenue outgrew peers that live on grants — Aceh's fiscal compact should climb the same three steps.
Three-stage fiscal-compact roadmap with peer benchmarks: Basque Country, Sarawak, and BARMM as the cautionary case, 2024–2026 figures.
STAGE 1 · 2026–2027 · THE RUU
Transfers, made permanent
Otsus 2.5% of DAU with no end date · migas DBH 70/30 confirmed · 30% ring-fenced into the Legacy Fund · Badan Koordinasi with outcome KPIs
PEER · BARMM (CAUTION)
Block grant of 5% of national internal revenue: PHP 83.4bn in 2025, PHP 337.5bn 2020–24 — yet low budget utilisation and elections postponed four times. Money without delivery capacity.
STAGE 2 · 2028–2032 · THE OMNIBUS
Assigned taxes and surcharges
Zakat as a PPh credit (Art 192) · Art 166 fiscal facilities finally issued · a provincial surcharge on selected national taxes collected in Aceh · port, zone, and tourism levies kept locally
PEER · SARAWAK
A 5% state sales tax on petroleum products since 2019 raised RM19.55bn by 2024; state revenue reached RM14.2bn in 2025. Won by ordinance and litigation, not by a new constitution.
STAGE 3 · 2033 ONWARD · THE COMPACT
Resource taxation, own collection
Aceh collects agreed taxes in its territory and remits a negotiated contribution for national services · Andaman gas revenue shared under a transparent formula · a fiscal council publishes the numbers
PEER · BASQUE COUNTRY
Collects its own income, corporate, VAT, and excise taxes — €18.31bn in 2024 — and pays Madrid a cupo of €1,532m for 2026, about 8%. The end state, not the first step.

SO WHAT
Stage 1 is the 2026 bill. Stages 2 and 3 are what the omnibus in 2027–28 must open. Each step raises Aceh's own revenue and lowers what Jakarta must transfer — a deal both sides can sign.
