STATE REVENUE VS PETROLEUM SALES TAX, RM BILLION
One state-set tax on one commodity flow now yields roughly a fifth of a record state budget. It is levied by Kuching, not transferred from Putrajaya.
OWN INSTITUTIONS BEHIND THE NUMBERS
TAX BASE
State sales-tax ordinance applied to petroleum products, 5%
COMPANY
Petros, the state oil and gas company; sole gas aggregator from 1 Mar 2025
BORDER
Own immigration desk: Section 66 endorsement for every non-resident entering
COURT
Sued Petronas over unpaid SST rather than wait for a federal formula
SO WHAT
A share is decided in the capital every budget cycle; a tax base is decided once and then collected. Aceh's post-2027 ask should include at least one assigned base, not only a DAU percentage.
