Passive first, active second, subsea third: a BUMD digital utility can earn on ducts and dark fibre before it needs any new law.
Proposed phased play with owning institution, estimated capital and KPIs, 2026–2031. Capital figures are estimates; targets are proposals.
PHASE 1 · 0–12 MONTHS
Passive: ducts and dark fibre
Incorporate Aceh Digital Utility as a Perseroda under the development corporation (PP 54/2017)
ADC SPV
Shared-duct qanun (SJUT) and one-window excavation and tower permits
Delivery Unit
Open-access duct and dark-fibre build, Banda Aceh to Lhokseumawe and inside KEK Arun; wholesale offer to the three MNOs
ADC SPV
Close the 149 blank-spot villages with BAKTI and SATRIA-1 backhaul under Ps 152
Delivery Unit
CAPITAL · Rp300–600B, Otsus-seeded (estimate)
PHASE 2 · 1–3 YEARS
Active: licence, IXP, data centre
Jartaptup licence through Komdigi OSS; Telsus licence for industrial networks at Arun and Sabang
ADC SPV
Aceh IXP with the APJII chapter inside a neutral 2–5 MW colocation centre in Banda Aceh
Invest Aceh
Government, school and clinic network plus public wifi as anchor tenant
Delivery Unit
Table the Sabang landing-site amendment to Kepmen KP 14/2021 with KKP and Komdigi
Delivery Unit
CAPITAL · Rp0.8–1.5T, KPBU-eligible (estimate)
