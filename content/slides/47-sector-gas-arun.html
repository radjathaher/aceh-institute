LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Ps 160–161 joint migas management through BPMA (PP 23/2015); Ps 181 revenue share of 70% for oil and gas; KEK Arun Lhokseumawe under PP 5/2017; MoU Helsinki 1.3.4 (70% of hydrocarbons).
BLOCKER
BPMA's authority ends at 12 nm and every discovery lies beyond it; POD-I (approved 9 Mar 2026) fixes 8 subsea wells, an FPSO and an 84 km pipeline, so onshore processing must be won before FID; the BPMA–SKK Migas MoU of 21 May 2026 gives Aceh coordination, not decision rights.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
File the Governor's formal onshore-processing position with ESDM and the operator before FID (the June 2026 request to delay the POD is the opening); convert the Indoasia Oiltank (Jul 2026), Dubai methanol (Apr 2026) and Jiangsu LNG (Jul 2026) interest into signed KEK tenant MoUs with land options; use the Presidential Envoy channel opened on 31 Jul 2026.
KPI · 5 YEARS
One onshore processing train at FID; three anchor tenants operating in KEK Arun; gas-linked formal jobs above the 2,415 baseline; migas DBH to Aceh rising from the 2026 trough (province DBH fell 58% to Rp136.9B).
SO WHAT
The gas will be produced either way. The only variable Aceh controls is where it is processed, and that window closes at FID. Land the molecules onshore and Arun becomes a platform; let them go to the FPSO and Aceh collects a royalty.
