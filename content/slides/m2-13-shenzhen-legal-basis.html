M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · SHENZHEN SEZ
M2-05-0
Shenzhen was empowered by a 1992 NPC Standing Committee decision to write its own economic regulations — an authorisation Beijing can revise at will.
Legal basis of the Shenzhen Special Economic Zone: 1980 baseline, 1992 legislative authorisation, 2020 comprehensive reform pilot.
STATUTE
NPC Standing Committee decision, 1 Jul 1992
Authorises Shenzhen to formulate its own SEZ regulations. The 2020 Comprehensive Reform Pilot added a list of 40 authorised items. Both are grants from the centre, not entitlements.
TIMELINE, 1980–2025
WHAT WAS DEVOLVED
Authority to formulate SEZ regulations that may vary from national law inside the authorisation (1992).
A named list of 40 reform items under the 2020 Comprehensive Reform Pilot — power by list, not by principle.
Not devolved: customs, immigration, a fiscal formula. The centre can narrow or revoke the authorisation at any time.
SO WHAT
Shenzhen's power came as a list, granted by the centre and revocable. That is the closest legal cousin to what Jakarta can give Aceh: an authorisation list with a time-box, not sovereignty.
