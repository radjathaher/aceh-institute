03 · WHAT UUPA ENACTED
S03-01
Aceh's special law sits one step below the Constitution, but its powers only work when Jakarta writes the regulations beneath it.
The binding legal hierarchy for Aceh, what each level controls, and the matters the Constitution keeps national.
LEVEL
INSTRUMENT
WHAT IT CONTROLS FOR ACEH
1
UUD 1945
Ps 18, 18A, 18B
Recognises special and distinctive regions inside a unitary state; relations between centre and regions are set by statute. The ceiling for everything below.
2
UU 11/2006 (UUPA)
273 articles · lex specialis
Powers, revenue shares, Otsus fund, courts, local parties, symbols. Calls for 9 PP and 3 Perpres; 5 PP and 3 Perpres issued by 2019.
3
PP and Perpres
PP 20/2007 · PP 3/2015 · PP 23/2015 · Perpres 75/2008 · 11/2010
Turn articles into working rules. A missing PP leaves the article inert: land (Ps 253), telecom licensing (Ps 151, 165), zakat as tax credit (Ps 192).
4
National sector laws
UU 23/2014 · UU 7/2017 · UU 3/2020 · Cipta Kerja
Later, general laws that ministries apply over UUPA. Article 8 consultation is meant to catch them; in practice it is skipped (UU 7/2017 revoked Ps 57 and 60 without the DPRA).
5
Qanun
Ps 235 · supervised by the centre
Aceh's own legislation, including Islamic-law fields. Qanun 17/2013 (KKR) shows the limit: Kemendagri asked for its revocation in November 2024.
6
Pergub, APBA, contracts
Governor regulations · budget · BUMD and PPP deals
Where delivery happens. Everything the Governor can do alone lives here; everything in rows 04 to 10 that needs Jakarta lives above.
KEPT NATIONAL
currency · immigration · spectrum · foreign policy · defence · general courts · national fiscal policy (UUPA Ps 7(2))
SO WHAT
UUPA is enforceable law, not a political text. But a UUPA article without its PP is a promise again — and later national laws are applied over it unless Aceh is consulted and objects in time.
