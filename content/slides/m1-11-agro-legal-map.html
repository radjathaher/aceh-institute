THE SPECIFIC BLOCKERS
Land titles. Perpres 23/2015 was signed and never executed; BPN's Kanwil was still operating in July 2026, so estate and plant sites cannot be assembled cleanly.
The export gate. Every port belongs to Pelindo and Kemenhub (Ps 172–173 unimplemented); the Penang Ro-Ro needs an RI–Malaysia bilateral.
Certification capacity. Halal, phytosanitary and traceability sit with national agencies whose Aceh desks are thin; no blocker in law, a blocker in throughput.
WHAT NEEDS NO NEW LAW
Cold stores, grading and processing centres, a certification desk and 12 nm fisheries licensing are all inside provincial powers and a development corporation today.
SO WHAT
Nothing in this sector waits on a new law. It waits on a landed-cost problem: cold chain, port and certification, all buildable now.
