M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · BARMM
M2-01-2
BARMM's lesson: a large automatic grant without an elected, capable government produces under-spending and four postponed elections.
What worked, what failed, and what transfers to Aceh, 2019–2026.
WHAT WORKED
Automatic 5% block grant with no annual negotiation — PHP 337.5bn paid in 2020–24.
Own parliament and Shari'ah courts inside a unitary state, upheld by the Supreme Court.
A joint national–regional review of the revenue share, run by the Department of Finance.
WHAT FAILED
The first parliamentary election was postponed four times, now 14 Sep 2026 — the transition never ended.
Budget utilisation fell low enough to trigger a national probe in 2025.
The territory was redrawn from outside: the Court removed Sulu in September 2024.
ACEH TRANSFER
WHAT ACEH COPIES
An automatic, formula-based transfer written into statute for the post-2027 period, released without annual negotiation.
A standing joint revenue-review body, with the national calculation published.
WHAT ACEH CANNOT COPY
A 5%-of-national-revenue scale: Indonesia budgets Otsus as a share of the DAU pool, not of total tax.
An appointed transition authority. Aceh already elects its government; the risk is the reverse — capacity, not legitimacy.
INSTRUMENT NEEDED IN INDONESIA
RUU UUPA Ps 183 with the earmark rule, plus a PMK fixing the formula and release schedule.
Badan Koordinasi Dana Otsus chaired by the Governor, reporting utilisation quarterly.
SO WHAT
Copy BARMM's formula, not its transition. Pair the 2027 formula with an execution body that publishes utilisation every quarter — the probe is the warning, not the model.
Source: PCO, election reset to Sep 2026; PNA, budget-utilisation probe; Supreme Court, Sulu ruling 9 Sep 2024; DOF revenue-share review. [905][527][901][904]
