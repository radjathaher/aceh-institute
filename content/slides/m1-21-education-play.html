M1 · SECTOR PLAYBOOK · EDUCATION, HEALTH AND TALENT
M1-07-2
Buy outcomes, not inputs: tie every Otsus rupiah for people to a placement, a licence or a tenant, and build two industry polytechnics inside the corridors.
Phased play with owner institution, capital order-of-magnitude (estimates) and proposed KPIs, 2026–2031.
PHASE 1 · 2026–2027
Measure and tie
OWNER
Pemerintah Aceh (BPSDM, Disdik) + USK
CAPITAL
~Rp300B/yr redirected from existing scholarships (estimate)
— Outcome contracts: scholarship paid on placement
— Tenant-signed skills map for Arun and Sabang
— Guru unggul programme with published results
PHASE 2 · 2028–2029
Build the pipeline
OWNER
Politeknik Lhokseumawe + KEK tenants + Kemdiktisaintek
CAPITAL
Rp1–2T from an Otsus vocational fund (estimate)
— Two industry polytechnic campuses: Arun, Sabang
— Finish the Otsus referral hospital as a teaching hospital
— Diaspora fellowship: 50 returnees a year
PHASE 3 · 2030–2031
Import expertise, export talent
OWNER
Aceh Talent Agency (new, under Aceh Development Corp)
CAPITAL
~Rp0.5T/yr from endowment yield (estimate)
— Maritime, sharia-finance and energy specialist schools
— Talent-visa lane in use for lecturers and engineers
— Aceh graduates placed in Malaysia and the Gulf
