M1 · SECTOR PLAYBOOK · RESILIENCE AND CLIMATE
M1-12-2
The resilience play screens every SPV project for climate risk and turns Otsus into a maintenance reserve, not more concrete.
Three-phase play with owner, capital order of magnitude and KPIs, 2026–2031. Targets are proposals.
THREE-PHASE PLAY · OWNER · CAPITAL (ORDER OF MAGNITUDE, ESTIMATE)
PHASE 1 · 2026–27
Rules before concrete
Owner: Bappeda Aceh with BPBA
~Rp200–500B, public
Climate-risk screen made mandatory for every SPV and Otsus project above a threshold.
Legacy Fund maintenance reserve ring-fenced inside the Otsus allocation.
Early-warning and disaster communications carried on the Aceh fibre backbone.
PHASE 2 · 2028–29
Basins and mangroves
Owner: Aceh Development Corp, resilience SPV
~Rp1–3T, public and KPBU
Flood-control KPBU on the worst-hit basins, Aceh Tamiang first.
Mangrove restoration with a blue-carbon registry route agreed with KLHK.
Coastal and floodplain rules enforced through the RTRW and permit conditions.
PHASE 3 · 2030–31
Revenue from resilience
Owner: resilience SPV with Kemenkeu and OJK
~Rp3–5T, blended
Blue-carbon revenue flowing into the Legacy Fund maintenance reserve.
First regional resilience bond tested under POJK 10/2024, a national first.
Insurance pool for public assets funded from the reserve and SPV revenues.
