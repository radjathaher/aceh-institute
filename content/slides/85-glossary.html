S12-02
Glossary: sixteen Indonesian legal and fiscal terms the deck uses without translation.
Bahasa Indonesia term, full form, and the English working meaning used in this deck.
UUPA
Undang-Undang Pemerintahan Aceh (UU 11/2006)
The Aceh Government Law: the statute that turned the 2005 Helsinki MoU into enforceable law.
Otsus
Dana Otonomi Khusus
Special-autonomy fund: 2% of national DAU 2008–2022, 1% 2023–2027, under UUPA Art 183.
DAU
Dana Alokasi Umum
General allocation grant from the centre; the national DAU pool is the base of the Otsus formula.
DBH
Dana Bagi Hasil
Revenue-sharing transfer (oil, gas, forestry, taxes); Aceh's 70% oil-and-gas share flows through this line.
PAD
Pendapatan Asli Daerah
Own-source revenue: local taxes, levies, BUMD dividends and, uniquely in Aceh, zakat.
APBA
Anggaran Pendapatan dan Belanja Aceh
The provincial budget, enacted each year by qanun (Qanun Aceh 1/2026 for 2026).
Qanun
Peraturan daerah Aceh
Provincial regulation; under UUPA it may carry Islamic-law (syariat) content, unlike an ordinary perda.
DPRA
Dewan Perwakilan Rakyat Aceh
Provincial legislature, 81 seats; the only one in Indonesia with local political parties.
BPMA
Badan Pengelola Migas Aceh (PP 23/2015)
Aceh oil-and-gas authority; joint management with the centre, capped at 12 nautical miles offshore.
KEK
Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus — Arun Lhokseumawe (PP 5/2017)
Special economic zone with tax and customs incentives; 2,600 ha around the former Arun LNG plant.
FTZ / BPKS
Kawasan Bebas Sabang / Badan Pengusahaan Kawasan Sabang (UU 37/2000)
Sabang free-trade and free-port zone and the authority that runs it.
KKR
Komisi Kebenaran dan Rekonsiliasi (Qanun Aceh 17/2013)
Truth and reconciliation commission for conflict-era violations; a UUPA Art 229 institution.
Wali Nanggroe
Lembaga Wali Nanggroe
Custodian of Acehnese custom, culture and unity; a UUPA institution with its own budget line.
Mahkamah Syar'iyah
Peradilan syariat Islam di Aceh
Islamic court for family, civil and jinayat cases, under the Supreme Court — not a separate jurisdiction.
Baitul Mal
Lembaga pengelola zakat, infak dan wakaf
Zakat and endowment authority; in Aceh zakat is provincial revenue (PAD) and a tax deduction.
BUMD / Perseroda
Badan Usaha Milik Daerah / Perseroan Daerah (PP 54/2017)
Regional state-owned enterprise; Perseroda is the limited-company form (region holds at least 51%).
Source: UU 11/2006; UU 1/2022 (HKPD); PP 54/2017 (BUMD); UU 37/2000 (Sabang); PP 5/2017 (KEK Arun); Qanun Aceh 17/2013 (KKR). [27]
