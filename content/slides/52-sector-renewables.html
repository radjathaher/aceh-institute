About 400 MW of headroom plus Andaman gas: industrial-tariff power for KEK Arun tenants and data centres, gas-to-power at Arun, solar on zone land, and a microgrid sandbox for Sabang and the 149 unconnected villages.
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Ps 156 (natural resources including energy) and Ps 189 (development corporations); Ps 160 gas allocation through BPMA; MoU Helsinki 1.3.4; the Mubadala–PLN EPI heads of agreement (Dec 2025) as the gas-to-power anchor.
BLOCKER
Tariffs and the single-buyer model are set nationally; no microgrid or captive-power sandbox regime exists; gas allocation for power depends on the POD design and on the 12 nm authority question; headroom without a firm industrial load is a cost, not an asset.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
Negotiate an industrial-tariff power purchase framework for Arun tenants with PLN, bundled with the KEK tenant MoUs; reserve zone land for solar; file the microgrid sandbox ask for Sabang alongside the private-5G ask; put a power-and-fiber-ready parcel in the Invest Aceh offer sheet.
KPI · 5 YEARS
At least 200 MW of contracted industrial load in the two corridors; renewable share of Aceh generation rising from the 59 MWp base; one microgrid pilot operating; power reliability reported for the zones.
SO WHAT
Power is the tenant's first question and Aceh can answer it today. A firm industrial tariff at Arun converts idle capacity into the cheapest investment incentive the province has, and no law needs to change first.
