Malacca traffic passes Aceh by — Malahayati moved 8,406 TEU in nine months while the Penang Ro-Ro waits on a bilateral letter.
Five-lane summary for logistics and Aceh–Penang/Andaman shipping: the Krueng Geukueh–Penang Ro-Ro and a port joint venture under UUPA Art 172-173.
OPPORTUNITY
8,406 TEU
Malahayati port, Jan-Sep 2025 (Pelindo)
Aug 2026
Aceh–Penang route work resumed after the Jan 2026 target slipped
A Krueng Geukueh–Penang Ro-Ro for Gayo coffee, fish and plant inputs; Sabang bunkering and repair; an Andaman feeder loop. The Strait carries the traffic; Aceh only needs a scheduled call.
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Art 172-173 (Aceh manages ports it builds; existing state facilities through joint management or joint venture); Art 189 (equity in state or private companies by qanun); Perpres 11/2010 (foreign cooperation).
BLOCKER
Every commercial port is run by Pelindo and Art 172-173 has never been executed (civil suit against the President and Minister of Transport filed 24 Sep 2025); the Ro-Ro needs an Indonesia–Malaysia bilateral, requested by letter to the President.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
Sign two term sheets: the Aceh–Penang route (vessel, schedule, cargo guarantees from coffee and fisheries shippers) and a Ports & Logistics SPV–Pelindo joint venture for Krueng Geukueh and Malahayati.
KPI · 5 YEARS
Weekly Penang Ro-Ro in service; Malahayati plus Krueng Geukueh above 50,000 TEU a year (target); port joint venture signed and operating; Aceh export cost per container published and falling.
SO WHAT
A joint venture with Pelindo is the fastest legal route to Art 172-173; a weekly Penang ferry is the fastest proof that Aceh sits on a trade lane, not beside one.
