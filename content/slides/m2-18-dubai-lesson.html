M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · DUBAI / DIFC / JAFZA
M2-06-2
Aceh can copy the zone-operator model now; a DIFC-style court needs a national statute and a Supreme Court delegation.
What worked and what failed in Dubai, and the instrument each lesson needs inside Indonesia's legal order.
WHAT WORKED
A higher law switched off ordinary rules inside a drawn perimeter, so investors knew exactly where the exception applied.
Port, bonded estate and one-stop licensing sat under one operator: 36% of GDP from a single fence.
Courts and regulator were credible to foreigners because they were foreign in form: common law, English, own judges.
WHAT FAILED OR STAYED OUT OF REACH
2009: the state holding borrowed against zone-inflated assets and needed a US$26bn standstill and federal support.
The model only exists because the federation agreed to carve out its own civil and commercial law. Indonesia's unitary order has no such lever below a UU.
SO WHAT
Build the operator now under provincial powers; ask Jakarta for the perimeter law and the court delegation as one package for Sabang and Arun.

INSTRUMENT NEEDED IN INDONESIA
One operator for port + estate + one-stop licensing
Copies now
Perseroda under PP 54/2017 using UUPA Ps 156 and 189; qanun to charter the Aceh Development Corporation
Bonded perimeter, 100% foreign ownership
Partly exists
Sabang FTZ (UU 37/2000) and KEK Arun (PP 5/2017) already carry customs status; extend by KEK/FTZ regulation, not new law
Own commercial court (DIFC Courts)
Cannot copy as-is
Judicial power sits under the Mahkamah Agung; needs a national statute plus MA delegation, as Mahkamah Syar'iyah shows. Nearer term: a commercial chamber + arbitration seat by qanun
Own financial regulator (DFSA)
Cannot copy
Financial regulation is national (OJK, BI); the realistic ask is an Islamic-finance sandbox delegated by national regulation
0% tax inside the fence
Blocked 20 years
UUPA Ps 166 fiscal facilities never received a PP or PMK; KEK holidays are national. Instrument: PP under Ps 166 inside the RUU package
