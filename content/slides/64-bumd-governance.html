A provincial company that pays Rp27-29B a year but has no business plan and 14 opaque subsidiaries is the pattern to end — six rules for every SPV.
Six-rule governance checklist scored against today's provincial BUMD pattern, as described by the DPRA special committee in September 2025.
#
RULE FOR EVERY SPV
WHAT IT MEANS IN PRACTICE
TODAY'S PATTERN
01
One mandate per company
Each SPV owns one asset class; no subsidiaries outside the mandate without a qanun.
NOT IN PLACE
02
Independent board majority
Directors recruited openly for skills; fixed terms; removal only for cause.
NOT IN PLACE
03
Audited, published accounts
Consolidated statements including every subsidiary, online within 120 days.
PARTIAL
04
A business plan the owner approves
Five-year plan with capital budget and return target, approved by the holding and DPRA.
NOT IN PLACE
05
KPI-linked management
Pay and renewal tied to published KPIs: return on assets, jobs, service uptime.
NOT IN PLACE
06
Perseroda form, outside capital up to 49%
Legal vehicle already available under PP 54/2017; operators can co-invest without control.
AVAILABLE
DIVIDEND PAID TO THE PROVINCE, RP BILLION
14
subsidiaries the DPRA committee could not see into, Sep 2025
0
approved business plans on file, per the same committee
SO WHAT
The lesson is structural, not personal: a wide mandate plus an appointed board produces opacity anywhere. Narrow the mandate and open the board before the first SPV is capitalised.
