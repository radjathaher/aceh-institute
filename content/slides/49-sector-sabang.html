A deep-water harbour at the mouth of the strait with cruise calls already in the log (Crystal Symphony 2025; Azamara Dec 2025 and Apr 2026). Plays: bunkering and ship-repair niche, cruise terminal, dive and marine tourism, fisheries processing, bonded distribution for the Penang link.
LEGAL BASIS
UU 37/2000 Sabang free trade and free port zone, BPKS as one-stop authority; UUPA Ps 172–173 port management and joint ventures; Perpres 11/2010 foreign cooperation; MoU Helsinki 1.3.5–1.3.7 (ports, free trade, direct sea access).
BLOCKER
No national parent body since the Dewan Kawasan lapsed; BPKS budget 2026 is Rp36.4B; land status unresolved; the customs exception stops at Sabang while Pelindo and InJourney run the mainland ports (LBH Kantara suit, 24 Sep 2025); the Penang Ro-Ro needs an RI–Malaysia bilateral.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
BPKS governance reset: professional board, a KPI contract with the Governor, a published concession pipeline; sign one anchor concession (bunkering and repair operator, or a cruise terminal operator) with a land option; carry the Penang Ro-Ro letter to the President with the Aug 2026 restart.
KPI · 5 YEARS
Non-tax and concession revenue at ten times the 2024 baseline; one anchor concession operating; at least twelve cruise calls a year; bunkering and repair volumes published quarterly; Penang route sailing.
SO WHAT
Sabang is a 26-year-old legal instrument that never got an operator. Hainan closed its customs perimeter island-wide in 2025; Sabang already has the perimeter and needs the governance. One concession that pays is worth more than another decade of budget.
