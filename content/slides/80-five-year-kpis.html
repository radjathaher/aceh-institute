HOW TO READ THE TARGETS
Targets are the author's proposals for a 2031 horizon, not commitments of the Government of Aceh. They are set to be testable, not comfortable.
Baselines are the latest verified figures: BPS Aceh for growth, poverty and employment; Qanun 1/2026 for the budget; DPMPTSP for investment; Komdigi for blank spots.
Three structural KPIs, capex share, PAD share and PMA share, move only if the institutions in row 08 exist. They are the early-warning set.
