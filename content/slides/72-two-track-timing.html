10 · THE JAKARTA PACKAGE
S10-02
Two tracks, not one bill: patch Otsus before the 2027 cliff, then draft an omnibus Aceh Governance & Free Port Act for 2027–28.
Track 1 is the RUU Perubahan UU 11/2006 now in DPR; Track 2 is a commissioned omnibus act with a rolling devolution commission; 2026–2029.
TWO TRACKS, 2026–2029 · QUARTERS
WHY TWO TRACKS
1
The patch cannot carry the package
The RUU changes 27 items and keeps "pertimbangan". Loading it with zone, customs and consent chapters would sink it before the cliff.
2
The omnibus needs proof first
A register, a compact and live SPVs in 2026–27 give Jakarta evidence that Aceh executes before it asks for a perimeter.
3
A commission outlives one bill
Jeju's rolling rounds and Hainan's zone law show the durable form is a standing body plus a zone chapter, not a single vote.
SO WHAT
Take the 2026 patch as it is, attach five conditions, and spend 2027 drafting the act that fixes what the patch cannot.
