M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 192 ZAKAT TAX CREDIT
M3-04
Zakat paid in Aceh should reduce income tax owed; after twenty years it only reduces taxable income.
Article 192 as written, the stalled Government Regulation, and the 2025 half-step from the Finance Ministry.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Article 192
“Zakat paid becomes a deducting factor against the amount of income tax owed by the taxpayer.” In tax terms this is a credit against tax due, not a deduction from taxable income.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
A PP on zakat as an income-tax credit (the RPP is drafted and stalled in Kemendagri harmonisation) plus a DJP implementing rule for Baitul Mal receipts.
STATUS
BLOCKED
Twenty years. PMK 114/2025 gives a gross-income deduction only.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Kemenkeu/DJP (tax base); Kemendagri (RPP harmonisation); Setneg (signature).
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
Sign the RPP as a PP (Setneg) with DJP guidance that Baitul Mal receipts credit against PPh due; MK 140/2025 has removed the constitutional objection.
SO WHAT
The credit turns Baitul Mal from a charity channel into a fiscal instrument Aceh controls. Twenty years of delay is a harmonisation choice, not a legal barrier.
