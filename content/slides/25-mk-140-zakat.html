03 · WHAT UUPA ENACTED
S03-06
In November 2025 the Constitutional Court confirmed that Aceh's special rules and national law run together — kekhususan is not a separate jurisdiction.
Decision 140/PUU-XXIII/2025 on zakat management (Article 44, UU 23/2011), rejected 13 November 2025, and what its reasoning means for every other Aceh-specific norm.
HOW THE COURT DESCRIBED THE TWO BODIES OF LAW
THE CASE
CASE
Putusan 140/PUU-XXIII/2025 — judicial review of Article 44, UU 23/2011 on zakat management, challenged for treating Aceh differently.
DECISION
Petition rejected, 13 November 2025. Aceh's kekhususan in zakat management is not contrary to the Constitution.
REASONING
Special rules differentiate Aceh's autonomy without creating a separate jurisdiction; Aceh-specific and national norms operate together inside one legal order.
USE
A shield against the "inconsistent with national law" objection to UUPA articles — and a ceiling: no Aceh rule can claim to exclude national law.
SO WHAT
Cite this ruling in every negotiation: the Court says Aceh's special norms and national norms coexist by design. That closes the inconsistency argument used against UUPA — and confirms that nothing in this deck needs a separate jurisdiction.
Source: Mahkamah Konstitusi, Putusan 140/PUU-XXIII/2025, 13 Nov 2025; MKRI press release, 13 Nov 2025. [114][115]
