Agriculture is 31% of Aceh's output and 41% of its jobs, but almost none of it leaves the province processed or certified.
Agro, fisheries and cold-chain play; GRDP share Q2 2026, employment share Feb 2026, Gayo coffee volumes 2025.
OPPORTUNITY
31% · 41%
agriculture share of GRDP (Q2 2026) and of jobs (Feb 2026)
35,000 t
Gayo coffee a year; 80% exported to 17 countries
12 nm
fisheries zone under Aceh's authority
Coffee and spices are already 11–24% of monthly exports while coal is 60–70%. Cold chain at landing sites, fisheries and coffee processing, export and halal certification, origin branding; patchouli (nilam) and cocoa as second-tier crops [verify volumes].
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Ps 156 (natural resources, fisheries within 12 nm) and Ps 189 (development corporations); MoU Helsinki 1.3.4 (all natural resources in territory and territorial sea); national framing under UU 23/2014 for the provincial fisheries zone.
BLOCKER
No export-grade cold chain or certification at landing sites; 64% of workers are informal; November 2025 floods cut Q4 growth to −1.61% and exports fell 6.9% in 2025; land administration is still run by the national BPN (Perpres 23/2015 never executed).
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
Cold-chain SPV under the Aceh Development Corp at one landing site with a signed processing offtaker; a Gayo origin brand with geographic-indication marketing; a certification desk (export, halal, organic) inside Invest Aceh; flood-resilience standards for the Arun cold-chain cluster.
KPI · 5 YEARS
Non-coal share of exports above 40%; three cold-chain hubs operating; processed fisheries export volume reported monthly; formal agri and food-processing jobs up; farm-gate share of the Gayo export price rising.
SO WHAT
This is the sector that touches 41% of households and needs no new law. A cold room, a certificate and a brand move value from the buyer in Medan or Penang to the farmer in Gayo; that is the fastest poverty lever Aceh owns.
