THE 100-DAY PATH
Day 0
Pergub signed: delivery unit of 12 staff under the Governor; register mandated as a public dataset.
Day 30
Register v1 published: all 13 gap-register articles with status, evidence and the ministry that holds the key.
Day 60
Draft Compact to Kemendagri and Setneg: one deadline per row, one named owner on each side.
Day 100
Central–Aceh Implementation Compact signed; register updated monthly; misses reported to DPRA and DPR.
SO WHAT
Twenty years of "not yet implemented" ends when every article has a status, a date and a name. A Pergub can do that without asking Jakarta for anything.
