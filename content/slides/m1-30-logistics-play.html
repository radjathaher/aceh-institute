M1 · SECTOR PLAYBOOK · LOGISTICS AND SHIPPING
M1-10-2
The logistics play starts with one weekly Ro-Ro to Penang and ends with Sabang selling bunker fuel to Malacca traffic.
Three-phase play with owner, capital order of magnitude and KPIs, 2026–2031. Targets are proposals.
THREE-PHASE PLAY · OWNER · CAPITAL (ORDER OF MAGNITUDE, ESTIMATE)
PHASE 1 · 2026–27
One sailing, one term sheet
Owner: Dishub Aceh with Invest Aceh
~Rp100–200B, mostly public
Weekly Ro-Ro Krueng Geukueh–Penang under Perpres 11/2010 once the bilateral is signed.
Port JV term sheet with Pelindo under Art 173, offered as a co-management pilot.
Sabang bunkering feasibility study with BPKS and a KPBU adviser.
PHASE 2 · 2028–29
Joint management
Owner: Aceh Development Corp, logistics SPV
~Rp1–2T, public and private
JV signed for Malahayati or Krueng Geukueh with Aceh holding an operating stake.
Sabang bunkering and ship-repair pilot delivered as a KPBU.
Cold-chain and Gayo coffee export lane to Penang on the Ro-Ro schedule.
PHASE 3 · 2030–31
Sabang on the Strait
Owner: logistics SPV with a foreign port partner
~Rp3–5T, mostly private
Sabang bunkering and transshipment niche serving Malacca Strait traffic.
Airport JV at Sultan Iskandar Muda under Art 173, using the port precedent.
Aceh–Andaman sea link study with India through the Perpres 11/2010 route.
