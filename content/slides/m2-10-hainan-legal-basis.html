M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · HAINAN FTP
M2-04-0
Hainan became a free trade port by a 2021 national law, and on 18 December 2025 the whole island went behind a single customs line.
Legal basis of the Hainan Free Trade Port: FTP Law 2021, State Council policy package, island-wide customs closure 2025.
STATUTE
Hainan Free Trade Port Law, June 2021
A national statute plus a State Council master plan and policy package. The customs line, tariff schedule and tax caps are all set in Beijing; the province implements.
TIMELINE, 2021–2025
WHAT WAS DEVOLVED
A special customs area covering the whole island: the "first line" to the world is open, the "second line" to the mainland is controlled.
Zero tariffs on 74% of lines; 15% corporate and personal income tax caps; provincial FTP regulations under the 2021 law.
Not devolved: the customs line, tariff schedule and tax caps are Beijing's; the 30% value-added rule polices goods crossing to the mainland.
SO WHAT
Hainan is a customs-line design: the whole island sits inside a bonded perimeter. Sabang lost its national parent body; only Jakarta can draw a customs line, so that is the specific ask.
