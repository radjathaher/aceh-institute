M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 160–161 MIGAS AND BPMA
M3-07
Joint gas management works onshore and out to 12 miles; the Andaman discoveries sit beyond that line.
Articles 160–161 mandate, PP 23/2015 and BPMA, and the 2026 push to extend Aceh's reach from 12 to 200 nautical miles.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Articles 160–161
“The Government and the Aceh Government jointly manage oil and gas resources located onshore and offshore within Aceh's area of authority.” They may form a joint implementing body; contracts and extensions need joint agreement.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
PP 23/2015 is issued and created BPMA. Reaching past 12 nm needs a PP amendment (or the RUU Art 160 clause) plus an ESDM decree redefining BPMA's working area.
STATUS
PARTIAL
Implemented to 12 nm; blocked beyond, where the Andaman gas is.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
ESDM; SKK Migas; Setneg for the PP amendment; contractors (Mubadala, Harbour Energy) for contract terms.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
A revised PP 23/2015 working-area clause (or the RUU Art 160 text) giving BPMA contract-party status for the Andaman blocks, with the 70% DBH formula applied to them.
SO WHAT
Every downstream plan at Arun assumes Andaman gas lands in Aceh on Aceh terms. The 12-mile line decides whether that gas is Aceh's or merely passes through.
