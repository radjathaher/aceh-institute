Aceh has the broadest autonomy in Indonesia and the deepest poverty in Sumatra — the fix is execution, not more autonomy on paper.
Executive summary: situation, complication, resolution. Figures are the latest verified releases as of 30 Aug 2026.
1
SITUATION
Aceh holds Indonesia's widest autonomy package and a two-decade peace.
Local parties, qanun and Islamic law, joint oil-and-gas management, a 70% migas share, a free-trade zone at Sabang and a special economic zone at Arun. The 2005 Helsinki MoU became law in UU 11/2006.

COMPLICATION
The money arrived; the powers did not — and the money stops in 2027.
Land, ports, tax facilities, zakat credit and telecom licensing were never handed over. Transfers fund 61% of APBA 2026 while capital spending is under Rp0.6T. The Otsus fund ends in 2027 under UUPA Art 183.

3
RESOLUTION
Execute UUPA as written, build two corridors, and rewrite the law once — as an omnibus.
01
Implementation register + compact with Jakarta: every UUPA article dated and owned.
02
Two corridors: Arun–Lhokseumawe (gas, chemicals, food) and Sabang–Banda (maritime, digital, tourism).
03
Omnibus rewrite 2027–28: permanent 2.5% DAU with conditions, consent restored, ports and 200 nm resources devolved.
SO WHAT
Transfer autonomy has run its course. Production autonomy is the only way the 2027 cliff becomes a launch pad.
