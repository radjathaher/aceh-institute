S07-07
Talent is the constraint every other sector play inherits — 64% of Aceh's workers are informal and the HDI sits at 76.2.
Five-lane summary for education, health and talent: opportunity, legal basis, blocker, first move within 12 months, five-year KPI.
OPPORTUNITY
76.23
HDI 2025, up from 75.36 in 2024
64.15%
of 2.50M workers in informal jobs, Feb 2026
Polytechnics contracted to KEK Arun and Sabang tenants; Universitas Syiah Kuala as research anchor; a diaspora-return programme; the RPJMA scholarship track [verify].
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Art 215-216 (education adapted to Aceh; right to quality schooling), Art 224 (health), Art 156(7) (resource operators must hire local people). RPJMA 2025-2029: 7 missions, 21 programmes, human capital among them.
BLOCKER
Training is not tied to tenant demand and scholarships are not tied to return. Otsus money went about 45% to infrastructure and about 10% to empowerment; agriculture still employs 41% of workers.
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
Tenant-linked vocational contracts: every KEK or SPV land deal carries a signed training-and-hiring schedule with a named polytechnic, funded from an Otsus vocational line.
KPI · 5 YEARS
Formal-job share from 36% to 45% (target); 5,000 tenant-contracted graduates placed; HDI at or above 79; every KEK tenant with a published local-hiring ratio.
SO WHAT
Every plant, port and data hall in this deck fails on staffing unless training is bought the way land is: by contract, per tenant, with a named school on the hook.
