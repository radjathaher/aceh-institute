M1 · SECTOR PLAYBOOK · MINING AND MINERALS
M1-09-2
The mining play is downstream and community-first: cement, aggregates and a 1% levy that is actually collected.
Three-phase play with owner, capital order of magnitude and KPIs, 2026–2031. Targets are proposals.
THREE-PHASE PLAY · OWNER · CAPITAL (ORDER OF MAGNITUDE, ESTIMATE)
PHASE 1 · 2026–27
Standards and the levy
Owner: Dinas ESDM Aceh with Invest Aceh
~Rp50–100B, mostly public
Qanun on the Art 159 levy with audited disbursement and a public register of payers.
Community-benefit and local-supplier standard attached to provincial land, roads and permits.
Kobexindo/Hongshi re-filed with Kemenperin on an agreed plant specification.
PHASE 2 · 2028–29
First downstream plant
Owner: Aceh Development Corp, minerals SPV
~Rp1–3T, public and private
Cement plant final investment decision with an outside partner at up to 49%.
Aggregates and clinker supply chain built around Aceh Selatan quarries.
Supplier-development fund financed from the levy, with published awards.
PHASE 3 · 2030–31
Diversify the buyers
Owner: minerals SPV with the port SPV
~Rp5–10T, mostly private
Second downstream line and cement exports through Malahayati or Krueng Geukueh.
Coal export diversification beyond India through the Penang and Andaman lanes.
IUP authority renegotiated under the revised UUPA and its implementing PP.
