02 · WHAT HELSINKI PROMISED
S02-05
On law and human rights, the MoU promised an independent judiciary and two institutions that were never built.
Clauses 1.4.1–1.4.5 and 2.1–2.3, with delivery status in 2026 from the UUPA gap register.
1.4.1
Separation of powers
Legislative, executive and judicial powers recognised as separate.
UUPA structures DPRA, Governor and courts as separate branches.
1.4.2
Aceh law on human-rights principles
Aceh's legislature redrafts Aceh's legal provisions on ICCPR and ICESCR principles.
Qanun-making power enacted; no rights audit of the qanun corpus.
1.4.3
Independent court system with appeals
An impartial, independent court system including a court of appeals, inside the Indonesian judiciary.
Mahkamah Syar'iyah under the Supreme Court (Ps 128); no Aceh-wide system.
1.4.4
Consent on police and prosecutor chiefs
Appointments need the Head of Aceh Government's consent; recruitment and training with consent.
Ps 205: Governor approval, 14-day silence counts as yes; one re-nomination.
1.4.5
Military civil crimes in civil courts
All civilian crimes committed by military personnel in Aceh tried in civil courts in Aceh.
Ps 203: tried "per prevailing legislation", i.e. military justice, in open session.
2.1
ICCPR and ICESCR
The Government of Indonesia adheres to both UN covenants.
Both covenants ratified by Indonesia in 2005 [verify].
2.2
Human Rights Court for Aceh
A Human Rights Court will be established for Aceh.
Ps 228 enacted; court never formed in 19 years (KontraS 2024).
2.3
Truth and Reconciliation Commission
A KKR for Aceh formed by the national commission.
Qanun 17/2013; 10,652 violations recorded; Kemendagri advised revoking the qanun, 7 Nov 2024.

SO WHAT
The rule-of-law promises were written into UUPA in 2006 and then left to national institutions to build. The two that needed a new body — the Human Rights Court and the KKR — are the two that never arrived, or arrived by qanun and are now asked to close.
Source: MoU Helsinki cl. 1.4 and 2; UU 11/2006 Ps 128, 203, 205, 228–229; KontraS 2024 and 2025; Antara, 7 Nov 2024. [100][102][108][119][109]
