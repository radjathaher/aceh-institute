Aceh still holds a 1% community-development levy the national mining law never removed — use it to turn a Rp10T cement plant into a supplier base.
Five-lane summary for mining, cement and downstream minerals: the Aceh Selatan cement MoU, licence centralisation, and a community-benefit standard.

mining GRDP, Q1 2026 year-on-year
Cement and limestone on the west coast, downstream minerals, and a local supplier base serving the plant and KEK Arun; mining is the one sector already contracting, so the floor is low.
LEGAL BASIS
UUPA Art 156 (Aceh manages non-renewable resources; operators must use local labour and inputs) and Art 159 (each mining operator funds community development at no less than 1% of the value of production sold each year).
BLOCKER
UU 3/2020 (Minerba) and PP 3/2015 centralise licensing; BKPM revoked 180 IUPs in Feb 2022 including Aceh permits; Aceh's letters on Minerba consultation went unanswered; the cement MoU is contested at ministry level [verify].
FIRST MOVE · 12 MO
A Pergub community-benefit standard: the Art 159 fund is agreed, collected, audited and published; a supplier-development schedule is attached to every IUP recommendation and KEK lease.
KPI · 5 YEARS
Art 159 fund collected and published for 100% of active producers; local suppliers at or above 30% of plant operating purchases; mining GRDP back to growth; cement plant in commercial operation.
SO WHAT
Licensing moved to Jakarta; the benefit rule did not. Aceh can set the price of a social licence today, before the first kiln is lit.
