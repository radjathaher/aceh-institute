One holding and six single-mandate SPVs — the Perseroda form lets Aceh keep 51% and bring in operators for the rest.
Org tree of Aceh Development Corporation under PP 54/2017. Each SPV owns one asset class and publishes audited accounts.
HOLDING · PERSERODA UNDER PP 54/2017
Aceh Development Corporation
Aceh holds at least 51%; outside shareholders up to 49%. Board appointed on merit; strategy and capital allocation only.
SPV 1
Industrial Land
Serviced plots at KEK Arun and Sabang; leases, not sales.
SPV 2
Digital Infrastructure
Open-access fibre, ducts, towers, exchange, data hall.
SPV 3
Ports & Logistics
JV partner to Pelindo; Penang route; cold-chain gateways.
SPV 4
Energy & Utilities
Gas offtake, solar, water and industrial power for tenants.
SPV 5
Food & Cold Chain
Fish, coffee and horticulture storage, processing, export.
SPV 6
Tourism Assets
Weh marina and moorings, Gayo lodges; concessions to operators.
Audited accounts for holding and every SPV, published within 120 days of year-end.
Independent directors form a majority of each board; no serving officials or party officers.
Published procurement: every contract above a threshold in open contracting data.

SO WHAT
Otsus money becomes an asset only if something owns it. Six narrow companies with outside partners are easier to audit and harder to capture than one broad one.
