M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 8 CONSULTATION
M3-06
Consultation with Aceh on national laws is mandated, procedured, and routinely skipped without consequence.
Article 8 mandate, the Perpres 75/2008 procedure, and the laws passed without DPRA consultation since.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Article 8
“Plans to form laws by the DPR that relate directly to the Government of Aceh shall be carried out with consultation and consideration of the DPRA.” The same rule covers international agreements and administrative policies; the procedure is set by Perpres.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
Perpres 75/2008 exists. Missing: a fixed consultation window, a duty to publish the response, and a DPR rules-of-procedure clause that makes skipping it visible.
STATUS
PARTIAL
Procedure exists; no sanction, no timeline, no published record.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
DPR (Baleg, tata tertib); Setneg and Kemenkum for the Perpres; the Constitutional Court as the only remedy.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
Write the consultation window and a publish-the-response duty into the RUU and the DPR rules of procedure; keep a public register of consultations requested versus answered.
SO WHAT
The MoU promised consent; the law gives consideration. Aceh will not get consent back, but a dated, published consultation record is a realistic and defensible ask.
