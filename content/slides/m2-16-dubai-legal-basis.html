M2-06-0
Dubai's zones work because higher law carved them out first: Jafza in 1985, DIFC by federal statute in 2004.
Legal instruments behind Jafza and DIFC, and the powers each one delegated to the zone, 1985–2025.
LEGAL INSTRUMENTS, TOP-DOWN
1985
Jebel Ali Free Zone (Jafza)
Customs-bonded perimeter beside Jebel Ali Port; 100% foreign ownership inside the fence.
2004
UAE federal financial free-zone framework + Dubai Law 9/2004
Creates DIFC; federal civil and commercial law is switched off inside the Centre; own regulator (DFSA).
2004
DIFC Courts
Independent common-law courts in English with their own judges, sitting inside a civil-law federation.
2021
Dubai Law 5/2021
Restates the DIFC law and consolidates the Centre's governance after 17 years of operation.
WHAT THE ZONE MAY DECIDE ITSELF

Courts

Ownership + customs

SO WHAT
Dubai did not get "more autonomy" in the abstract. A higher law ring-fenced a perimeter and let the zone write its own rulebook inside it. That is the shape of the ask for Sabang and Arun.
