WHAT ACEH CAN DO NOW
Mandate a climate-risk screen for every SPV and Otsus project above a set threshold.
Ring-fence a maintenance reserve inside the Otsus allocation (Legacy Fund) so repairs are funded before new ribbons.
Put mangrove restoration and early warning into the RTRW and the first KPBU pipeline.
SO WHAT
Nothing here waits for a new law. Resilience is a budgeting rule and a planning rule the Governor can sign this year.
