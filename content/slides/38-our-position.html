05 · OTSUS AND THE 2027 CLIFF
S05-04
Aceh should ask for 2.5% of DAU permanently — and attach five accountability conditions that Jakarta cannot reasonably refuse.
Our position on Article 183: the ask, the five conditions that travel with it, and the domestic precedent.
THE ASK — ARTICLE 183
2.5%
of DAU, permanent — and accountable
Roughly Rp10 trillion a year at the 2026 DAU base (derived from Rp4.00T at 1%), against Rp4.15T proposed for 2027.
Sunset reviews apply to programmes every five years — never to the transfer itself.
FIVE CONDITIONS ACEH OFFERS WITH THE ASK
1
Ring-fenced legacy and capital share
A fixed share (30%) goes to the Aceh Legacy Fund and capital assets, by law, every year
2
Independent Otsus body
The Badan Koordinasi in the DPR text, with a professional secretariat and a board Jakarta helps appoint
3
Outcome KPIs, not disbursement rates
Poverty, formal jobs, learning and health outcomes — published against every programme
4
Open dashboards and contracts
Every Otsus project, contract, and BPK finding visible in public within 30 days
5
Five-year sunset review of programmes
Programmes that miss their KPIs are closed or redesigned; the transfer itself is never in question
PRECEDENT · PAPUA
2.25% to 2041
UU 2/2021 renewed Papua's Otsus at 2.25% of DAU for twenty more years, with earmarks and a supervisory body. Jakarta has already signed a formula of this shape.
LOCAL SUPPORT
The Aceh Institute policy paper (Sep 2025) proposes the same bundle: a Papua-style extension, an independent managing body, and a shift to productive investment.
SO WHAT
Permanence is easier to grant when the money is visibly governed. Aceh offers the governance first.
