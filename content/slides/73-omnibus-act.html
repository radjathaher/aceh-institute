10 · THE JAKARTA PACKAGE
S10-03
One omnibus act with six chapters turns the MoU's unkept promises into enforceable procedure.
Proposed structure of the Aceh Governance & Free Port Act: six chapters, each paired with the precedent it borrows from; proposal 2026.
ACEH GOVERNANCE & FREE PORT ACT · SIX CHAPTERS
CHAPTER 1
Consent restored
Consultation and consent of DPRA and Governor for every Aceh-related national measure, as MoU 1.1.2(b)–(d) promised, with timelines and written reasons.
Precedent: MoU Helsinki 2005; Perpres 75/2008 procedure
CHAPTER 2
Devolution commission
A standing Central–Aceh commission that issues devolution packages on a fixed cycle, each with an implementing PP deadline and a sunset review.
Precedent: Jeju Special Act 2006, 7 rounds; Shenzhen 2020 pilot
CHAPTER 3
Free Port & Industrial Transformation
Sabang, Arun and logistics areas as one perimeter: rules of origin, bonded movement, zone tax, customs procedure, talent fast-track.
Precedent: Hainan FTP Law 2021; UU 37/2000 Sabang; PP 5/2017 Arun
CHAPTER 4
Fiscal compact
Assigned taxes, resource taxation, a transparent calculation of central revenue from Aceh, and an independent fiscal council that publishes it.
Precedent: Concierto Económico Law 12/2002; Sarawak SST 2019
CHAPTER 5
Regulatory sandboxes
Time-boxed sandboxes for private 5G and community internet, Islamic fintech, digital port and customs, microgrids, carbon and blue economy.
Precedent: Komdigi community-internet sandbox (2025); OJK sandbox
CHAPTER 6
Commercial chamber + arbitration
A specialised commercial chamber at PN Banda Aceh and a recognised arbitration seat for zone contracts, under the Supreme Court, not beside it.
Precedent: DIFC Courts 2004 (form adapted); UU 30/1999 arbitration
HOW THE CHAPTERS FIT
Chapters 1, 2 and 4 are governance; 3, 5 and 6 are the economic perimeter. Each chapter carries its own implementing-regulation deadline so that no article waits 20 years for a PP, as Art 192 has.
SO WHAT
Every chapter has a foreign precedent and a domestic hook already in UUPA. Nothing here needs a constitutional amendment.
