M2 · PEER CASE STUDY · SINGAPORE EDB / JTC
M2-08-2
Aceh can build an EDB-style Invest Aceh and a JTC-style land corporation under UUPA Ps 156 and 189 today.
EDB and JTC design features Aceh can copy today, the ones it cannot, and the provincial instrument for each.
WHAT WORKED
Single-purpose statutory boards with a mandate, a board and a balance sheet, unchanged in form since 1961 and 1968.
Land treated as an instrument the agency owns and leases, not a permit the investor must assemble.
One voice abroad: 22 offices sell the same offer, and the account manager at home closes it.
WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER
Singapore is a sovereign city-state. Its tax, customs and immigration levers are not available to any Indonesian province, and this deck does not pretend otherwise.
EDB's incentives are national fiscal tools. Aceh's equivalent, UUPA Ps 166, has waited 20 years for a PP.
SO WHAT
Invest Aceh and the Aceh Development Corporation are the two institutions Aceh can legislate itself this year. Everything else on the Singapore list belongs to Jakarta, and should be asked for as such.
