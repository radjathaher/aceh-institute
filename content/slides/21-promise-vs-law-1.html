03 · WHAT UUPA ENACTED
S03-02
Where the MoU said consent, own taxes and own loans, UUPA wrote consideration, national tax law and Finance Ministry approval.
Four comparison lanes: the MoU clause on the left, the UUPA article that implements it on the right, with the gap marked.

GAP
UU 11/2006, 1 AUG 2006
1.1.2 (a) · GENERAL AUTHORITY
All public sectors, except six: foreign affairs, external defence, national security, monetary and fiscal matters, justice, freedom of religion.
narrowed
Ps 7 (1)–(2)
Same six exceptions, plus an open seventh: "urusan pemerintahan yang bersifat nasional", defined later by PP 3/2015 (foreign investment, cross-province, beyond 12 nm).
1.1.2 (b)–(d) · CONSENT
International agreements, DPR decisions and administrative policies on Aceh "dengan konsultasi dan persetujuan" of the DPRA or the Head of Aceh Government.
consent removed
Ps 8 (1)–(4)
"dengan konsultasi dan pertimbangan" — an opinion, not a veto; procedure by Perpres (75/2008). UU 7/2017 revoked Ps 57 and 60 with no DPRA consultation at all (MK 61/PUU-XV/2017).
1.3.2 · TAXES
"Aceh berhak menetapkan dan memungut pajak daerah" to fund its own activities; trade and attract investment directly.
narrowed
Ps 180
PAD = regional taxes, levies, separated assets, zakat and other lawful income — within national tax law. No own income, corporate or customs code; zakat-as-tax-credit (Ps 192) still without its PP.
1.3.1 · BORROWING AND INTEREST
Funds through external loans; the right to set interest rates different from those of Bank Indonesia.
rate power gone
Ps 186
Foreign-sourced loans only through the central government, with Finance Minister approval after Home Affairs consideration; domestic loans with Home Affairs consideration. Interest-rate power: absent.

SO WHAT
UUPA kept the shape of the MoU and changed the verbs. Every narrowed lane is a lane the 2026 revision can widen — Article 8 first, because consent is what protects all the others.
