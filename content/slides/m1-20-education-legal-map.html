THE ASK, WITH PRECEDENT
1
Fast-track approval for corridor-linked polytechnic programmes. Precedent: Jeju's 4,660 devolved powers include its own institutions.
2
Convert the Otsus people earmark into an endowment and a vocational fund. Precedent: Aceh Institute paper (Sep 2025) on productive Otsus.
3
A talent-entry lane for diaspora and foreign lecturers. Precedent: Jeju's visa-free entry under its 2006 Special Act.
SO WHAT
Aceh's education power is real but stops at the school gate. Above it every approval is national — so the ask is speed, not sovereignty.
