THE ASK, WITH PRECEDENT
1
A destination-management concession for Weh — BPKS plus one private operator. Precedent: Jeju Investment Promotion Zones (44 sites, tax holidays).
2
Sabang as a cruise home-port with immigration and customs on arrival. Precedent: Sabang already holds free-zone customs status (UU 37/2000).
3
The RI–Malaysia bilateral for the Aceh–Penang Ro-Ro by 2027. Precedent: Perpres 11/2010 foreign-cooperation right; talks resumed Aug 2026.
SO WHAT
Aceh's only tourism power is the destination itself. Every gate is national — so the ask is three gates opened, not a new law.
