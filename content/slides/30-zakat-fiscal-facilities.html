04 · THE IMPLEMENTATION GAP
S04-04
Two fiscal promises in the UUPA — zakat as a tax credit and special fiscal facilities — have waited twenty years for a rule that never came.
Articles 192 and 166: instrument required, instrument issued, years elapsed, and what the 2026 revision does with each.
ARTICLE 192 — ZAKAT AS INCOME-TAX CREDIT
20
years waiting for
a Government Regulation
REQUIRED
PP making zakat paid in Aceh a credit against PPh
ISSUED
None. RPP stuck at Kemendagri (2024). PMK 114/2025 gives only a gross-income deduction, as everywhere else
RUU 2026
Re-inserts Art 192 as “zakat deductible” — still not a credit
ARTICLE 166 — SPECIAL FISCAL FACILITIES
20
years without any
instrument at all
REQUIRED
Fiscal facilities for Aceh set by the Ministry of Finance
ISSUED
No PMK, no PP, no Perpres found in twenty years
RUU 2026
Not on the revision list at all (touches 160, 165, 183, 192)
SO WHAT
Both promises need one signature each, not a new law. Put Art 166 back into the RUU and ask for the zakat PP as a dated deliverable in the implementation compact.
Source: Baitul Mal Aceh Utara, 29 Sep 2024; RRI / Kanwil DJP Aceh, 2025; Pikiran Rakyat Aceh; Dialeksis, 2026. [208][209][210][207]
