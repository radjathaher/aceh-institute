PILLARS AND PLAYERS
Baitul Mal Aceh + 23 kab/kota
Collects zakat and infaq as provincial revenue (UUPA Art 180, 191); kekhususan affirmed by MK 140/2025.
Bank Aceh Syariah + Qanun LKS 11/2018
Aceh is the only province where financial institutions must operate on sharia principles.
Mahkamah Syar'iyah
Own sharia court tier under the Supreme Court; can hear Islamic commercial disputes.
KEK Arun (2,600 ha) + Sabang FTZ
Serviced zones with tax status, but no halal-manufacturing tenant yet (proposal).
Capital-market and fintech rails
Sukuk daerah unused; no regional Islamic-fintech sandbox; Art 192 credit blocked.
SO WHAT
The identity is real and legally protected; the money rails are switched off. A sukuk, the zakat credit and a fintech sandbox turn syariat from a rule into a market.
