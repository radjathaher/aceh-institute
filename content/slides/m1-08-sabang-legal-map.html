THE SPECIFIC BLOCKERS
No owner. Since the Dewan Kawasan lapsed, BPKS reports to nobody in Jakarta and only informally to the Governor; a Rp36.4B budget cannot run a free port.
Ports are not Aceh's. Pelindo and Kemenhub run every port; the LBH Kantara suit against the President and Menhub (24 Sep 2025) is the live test of Ps 172–173.
Land and routes. Zone land titles are unresolved (Wali Nanggroe inventory, Jul 2026) and the Penang Ro-Ro waits on a bilateral letter to the President.
ASK, WITH PRECEDENT
Re-parent BPKS under the Governor with a delivery compact, then a Hainan-style free-port act: island-wide customs closure took Hainan from law (2021) to closure (18 Dec 2025).
SO WHAT
Sabang does not need new powers first; it needs one accountable owner. Ask for the owner before asking for more.
