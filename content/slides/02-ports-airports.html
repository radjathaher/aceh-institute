M3 · GAP REGISTER · ART 172–173 PORTS AND AIRPORTS
M3-02
Every commercial port and airport in Aceh is still run by a BUMN; the joint-management clause has never been used.
Articles 172–173 mandate, the later sector laws that override it, and the 2025 lawsuit that now tests it.
THE MANDATE · UU 11/2006
Articles 172–173
“Public ports and airports managed by a BUMN when this Law is enacted shall have their management cooperated with the Aceh Government and/or kabupaten/kota … which may take the form of a joint venture company.” Aceh may also build and run its own.
THE INSTRUMENT NEEDED
JV term sheets with Pelindo (ports) and InJourney Airports (SIM, Malikussaleh) under Kemenhub concession rules, reconciled with UU 23/2014 and PP 31/2021.
STATUS
BLOCKED
Overridden by later sector law; now in litigation.
WHO HOLDS THE KEY
Kemenhub (concessions); Pelindo and InJourney as BUMN operators; Kementerian BUMN; the courts.
EVIDENCE TIMELINE
WHAT UNBLOCKS IT
One negotiated JV term sheet (Malahayati or Krueng Geukueh with Pelindo) as precedent, backed by the RUU clause and a Kemenhub circular treating Art 173 as lex specialis.
SO WHAT
Aceh's Penang Ro-Ro, Sabang free port and Arun corridor all pass through gates Aceh does not hold. A single JV precedent is worth more than a court win.
