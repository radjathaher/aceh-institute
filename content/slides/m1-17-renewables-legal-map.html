THE ASK, WITH PRECEDENT
1
A business area (wilayah usaha) for an Aceh utility at KEK Arun and Sabang. Precedent: zones and estates elsewhere in Indonesia hold their own [verify].
2
An Andaman gas allocation priced for Aceh industry. Precedent: PP 23/2015 joint management; the Governor's onshore-at-Arun request, Jun 2026.
3
An ESDM microgrid and renewables sandbox for Sabang, Simeulue and Gayo. Precedent: Komdigi's community-internet sandbox (2025).
SO WHAT
Aceh cannot legislate a tariff, but it can own a business area and a wire. That is the one lever ESDM already hands to zones.
